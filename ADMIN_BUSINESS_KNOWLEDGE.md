### Dashboard > Main Admin Dashboard

- **Purpose:** Summarizes sales, order demand, popular items, branch activity and team indicators; panels use different periods and inclusion rules.
- **Branch scope:** Normally the selected branch. Branch Admins are restricted to their assigned branch. Branch performance can compare all active branches accessible to an Admin, even when the other panels show one branch.
- **Period scope:** The sales chart defaults to the last 30 days; its range changes only that chart and its totals/comparison. Headline cards, best sellers, order volume and team indicators retain their own periods.

### Dashboard > Shared Sales Meaning

- **Revenue / sales:** Value of orders currently marked completed or delivered, attributed to order creation date, regardless of payment date or recorded collection. All other order statuses are excluded; historical totals can change after status, discount or charge changes.
- **Order value:** Sum of non-cancelled line quantities × recorded order-time prices, plus delivery fee for delivery orders and the linked fee/prepaid reservation charge; then apply the order discount percentage to the entire subtotal. Menu items and platters are included; older lines without recorded prices use current menu/platter prices.
- **Reservation charge:** Reserved duration rounded up to whole hours × current table hourly rate, rather than the recorded reservation amount; zero duration gives zero. Free reservations add nothing. Reservation payments are not subtracted; a linked charge is included on each qualifying order without reservation-status filtering.
- **Adjustments:** No separate tax, service charge, refund or payment adjustment is applied; the stored discount amount is not an additional deduction.
- **Interpretation:** These are operational order values, not verified cash collections or profit. The dashboard does not establish expenses, supplier payments, outstanding liabilities or net profit.

### Dashboard > Headline and Side Summary Cards

- **Menu Items:** Current count of menu-item records in scope, including unavailable items; excludes platters. The availability caption does not establish how many items can currently be sold.
- **Total Revenue / Total sales:** Same monthly revenue figure repeated in the headline and side card, using Shared Sales Meaning and the fixed-summary date rules below; do not add them together.
- **Total Orders:** Number of orders created since the month began, including pending, cancelled and every other status. Today and This week are subsets of those monthly orders, with the same status inclusion.
- **Total Sold Products:** Sum of order-line quantities since the month began, including menu items, platters, cancelled lines and orders of every status. A platter counts as ordered platter units, not its component portions. Counts ordered quantities, not distinct products or confirmed completed sales.
- **Display:** Monetary headline, side and best-seller figures are rounded to whole AFN; sales-chart amounts show up to two decimals. Icon rings on headline cards and the upward icon beside monthly sales are decorative, not measured percentages or evidence of growth.

### Dashboard > Sales Trend and Selected-Period Summary

- **Date inclusion:** Both selected dates are inclusive, using Asia/Kabul calendar days and order creation time. Completion, delivery and payment dates do not determine inclusion. Completed/delivered statuses and Shared Sales Meaning apply throughout.
- **Presets:** Last 7/30/90 days include today and the preceding 6/29/89 days. This month/year starts on the first calendar day and ends today; Last month/year covers the entire preceding calendar month/year. Last 12 months starts the day after the corresponding prior-year date, adjusted for leap days, and ends today. Future end dates are excluded.
- **Total revenue:** Sum of qualifying order values in the selected period, in AFN.
- **Completed orders:** Count of qualifying completed or delivered orders; differs from the all-status headline Total Orders.
- **Average order value:** Selected revenue ÷ qualifying order count; zero when there are no qualifying orders. Indicates average order value, not customer spend per visit or margin.
- **Revenue per day:** Selected revenue ÷ all calendar days in the inclusive range, including zero-sales days and today if selected.
- **Revenue / Orders graph:** Each point totals the chosen metric within its displayed day, week or month; tooltips also show qualifying order count and average order value for that bucket. Empty dates/buckets are zero. Weekly buckets follow Monday–Sunday and monthly buckets follow calendar months, clipped to the selected boundaries.
- **Automatic grouping:** Daily for up to 62 days, weekly for 63–180, monthly above 180. Grouping changes the plotted buckets, not period totals. Expanded-graph zoom changes only the visible points; summary totals and comparison still cover the full selected range.
- **Change versus previous period:** Compares the active graph metric with the immediately preceding, equal-length calendar period: (selected − previous) ÷ previous × 100, rounded to one decimal. When the previous value is zero, no percentage is calculated; “No sales in previous period” can also mean zero prior revenue despite qualifying orders. A range including today compares an unfinished day with a complete historical day.

### Dashboard > Best Selling Items

- **Purpose / Sold:** Up to four entries ranked by summed order-line quantity for Today, Week or Month; Month is the default. Includes every order and line status, including cancellations; Week is not clipped to the current month.
- **Price / Revenue:** Price is the current menu price; entry revenue is quantity × that price, without order discounts, delivery or reservation charges. It does not use recorded order-time prices and can change when menu prices change.
- **Grouping / platters:** Entries with the same menu name, current price and image are combined. Platter-only lines contribute quantity to an unnamed entry, with missing price/revenue displayed as zero; they are not separately identified or correctly valued here.
- **Interpretation:** Shows recorded ordering popularity under these rules, not verified completed-sales popularity or profitability. Its revenue does not reconcile directly to dashboard sales revenue.

### Dashboard > Order Volume, Branch and Delivery Charts

- **Order volume:** Counts completed/delivered orders by creation day for the latest 14 dates with qualifying activity from the fixed-summary lookback; dates without qualifying orders are omitted. This is not necessarily the last 14 consecutive calendar days.
- **Side sparklines:** Revenue and completed/delivered order counts for the latest 10 qualifying-activity dates from the same lookback; independent of the selected sales-chart range.
- **Branch performance:** Appears when more than one comparison branch is available. Compares monthly completed/delivered order revenue by branch using Shared Sales Meaning; branch boundaries use Kabul dates and can differ from headline revenue boundaries.
- **Delivery activity:** Appears instead when at most one comparison branch is available. Today, This week and This month count delivery-type orders created in those periods, including cancelled and unfinished deliveries; they do not measure successful deliveries, speed or driver performance. Periods overlap and must not be added together.

### Dashboard > Fixed-Summary Date Rules

- **Calendar mismatch:** Fixed-summary Today and month starts are chosen using the UTC date. Order/item count cutoffs and delivery dates use Kabul boundaries, while headline revenue and small trend charts use UTC creation dates. Attendance uses the summary's Today date. During 00:00–04:29 Kabul time, summary Today can still be yesterday; month boundaries can also differ from the selected sales chart.
- **This week:** Starts seven dates before summary Today, rather than at the calendar week's start. Headline order and delivery counts are additionally restricted to the current month, so early-month Week excludes the previous month's days; best sellers are not restricted this way.
- **Trend lookback:** Includes qualifying orders created on/after the Kabul date 30 dates before summary Today, grouped by UTC day; this is not an exact 30-day chart. Fixed summaries apply start cutoffs without an upper-date cutoff, unlike the bounded selected-period sales chart.
- **Unresolved:** Whether these differing day boundaries, week definitions and status inclusions match the intended business policy is unverified; they describe current calculations.

### Dashboard > Team Overview and Recent Activity

- **Staff:** Current count of staff records assigned to the branch, or restaurant staff in an all-branch summary; includes inactive and resigned staff, without a hire-date filter.
- **Attendance today:** Number of Present attendance records dated summary Today ÷ Staff × 100; zero if Staff is zero. Counts shift records, not unique people or scheduled staff; multiple shifts can inflate the rate. The displayed gauge is capped at 100% and rounded to a whole percent, so it is not a reliable unique-person attendance rate.
- **Rating:** All-time arithmetic mean of review ratings in scope on a 1–5 scale, displayed to one decimal; zero means no reviews. It combines menu-item and delivery reviews and is not today's satisfaction score.
- **Recent activity / notifications:** Up to four latest unread notifications from the latest ten returned, including restaurant-wide notifications within a branch view. Timestamps are notification creation times; this panel is a limited unread activity feed, not a complete transaction history. An empty panel can also result from a loading failure.

### Operations > Shared Interpretation

- **Scope:** Operations pages normally show the selected branch. Counts, charges and statuses reflect current records; a historical date filter does not reconstruct their values as they stood then. Screen timestamps follow the device's timezone; timestamp-based date filters use Asia/Kabul.
- **Money:** Amounts are AFN. The shared order-value and reservation-charge calculations are defined under Dashboard > Shared Sales Meaning; page-specific differences below take precedence.

### Operations > Orders

- **Purpose:** Reviews dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders, their service progress, current charges and recorded payment information.
- **Period:** Defaults to Today. Today/Yesterday use Kabul calendar dates; Last 7 days includes today plus six preceding days; This month runs from the first through today; All time removes the date restriction. Custom boundaries are inclusive and filter order creation date, not payment or completion date; future end dates are disallowed.
- **Total Orders / Pending / Completed:** Counts for the selected branch and period before search/status filtering or pagination. Total includes every status; Pending counts only pending; Completed counts only completed, excluding delivered. Pending plus Completed need not equal Total. Search/status filters narrow the rows and pagination count, not these three cards.
- **Statuses:** Pending = awaiting preparation; In progress = preparation started; Ready = kitchen work ready; Served = dine-in served; Picked up = takeaway handoff; Out for delivery = delivery in transit; Completed/Delivered = finalized orders; Cancelled = cancelled record. Service progress alone does not establish collection.
- **Total / line subtotal:** Total follows the shared order-value calculation for any listed status, including cancelled orders; it is not automatically reportable sales. A line subtotal is quantity × recorded order price, with the legacy current-price fallback; cancelled lines remain visible but are excluded from Total. Delivery fee and applied discount percentage appear separately in details.
- **Balance Due / Post-paid:** max(Total − linked reservation prepayment, 0), displayed only when positive. The same figure is relabelled Post-paid for completed orders; it is not reduced by a separately recorded order payment and may remain positive after completion. The reservation-payment panel uses the reservation balance described below, not this whole-order balance.
- **Received by / Paid at:** Staff identity and timestamp recorded on completion/delivery. Bill printing alone does not record payment. “Not paid yet” is shown when the receiver's displayed full name is missing, which can also reflect an unnamed staff account; it is not conclusive payment evidence.
- **Preparation time:** Rounded minutes between the first recorded preparation start and ready time; absent until both exist. Excludes time awaiting preparation and delivery time.

### Operations > Tables

- **Purpose:** Current seating capacity, table state, active orders and current/next bookings; not a dated occupancy or table-revenue report.
- **Tables / Seats:** Number of table records and sum of their capacities, including unavailable tables. Status-chip counts cover all loaded tables; name search and the selected chip affect visible cards, not these totals. Seats are configured capacity, not occupied guest count.
- **Available / Occupied / Unavailable:** Stored table states indicating availability, occupation or manual unavailability. An active order normally marks its table occupied; finalization releases it. These are table counts, not seat counts or utilization percentages.
- **Reserved:** Displayed whenever there is a current reservation, overriding the stored state even if occupied/unavailable. Reserved bookings are current within their start/end window; Arrived bookings are current until their end, even if their scheduled start is still ahead. Elapsed bookings can disappear from the current display without their stored status changing.
- **Next reservation:** Earliest future booking still marked reserved; it does not by itself make the table currently Reserved. End time is start plus reserved duration.
- **Active order / Items:** Most recently created order at the table in pending, in-progress, ready or served status. Only one active order is displayed, not an aggregate if duplicate assignments exist. Items counts non-cancelled order lines, not summed portions or distinct menu products. Its loaded amount follows the shared order value.
- **Amount limitation:** Some live item updates use current menu prices and omit platter values, discounts and extra charges; the table amount can temporarily differ from order details. Reconcile against refreshed order details before treating it as the payable amount.

### Operations > Reservations

- **Purpose / dates:** Reviews bookings, guest demand, scheduled table time and reservation charges. Start/end filters apply inclusively to the reservation date, not record creation, arrival or payment date; no default date/status restriction. Pagination count includes all matching bookings, not only the visible page.
- **Guests / duration:** Guests is the recorded party size, not confirmed diners. End time = start time + duration; duration is reserved time, not measured stay. Standard booking checks compare party size with table capacity and prevent overlapping reserved/arrived bookings for the same table and reservation date; adjacent time slots are allowed.
- **Statuses:** Reserved = booked; Arrived = arrival recorded; Completed = marked finished, including completion alongside a matching order; Cancelled/No show = recorded cancellation/non-attendance. Passage of time alone does not establish arrival, completion or no-show.
- **Free / Fee / Prepaid:** Free adds no reservation charge to the order; Fee and Prepaid use the shared duration/rate charge. Prepaid identifies the reservation type; the actual recorded prepayment is a separate amount and need not equal the full charge.
- **Total price / recorded amount:** Displayed Total price uses the shared duration/current-table-rate calculation, even for Free reservations. The separately recorded amount is set to zero for Free, otherwise calculated when the booking is created/edited; later table-rate changes can make it differ from displayed Total price.
- **Pre-paid amount:** Payment amount recorded against the booking, independent of a later order payment. It is not a sum of collections in the selected booking-date period.
- **Balance Due / Post-paid:** Displayed Total price minus Pre-paid amount, without a zero floor; negative means recorded prepayment exceeds this calculated charge. Completed only changes the label to Post-paid; it does not record an additional payment or clear the balance. Free bookings can show a misleading positive balance because displayed Total price ignores the free type.
- **Financial limitation:** Cancellation/no-show does not automatically refund prepayment. Displayed reservation balances and whole-order balances use different formulas; do not add them as separate debts. Reconciliation of free-booking displays and actual charges remains unresolved.

### Operations > Daily Productions

- **Purpose / period:** Tracks prepared portions for bulk-cooked menu items, separate from raw ingredient stock. One current batch per menu item and branch persists until changed or cleared; “Daily” does not mean a midnight reset or production within today's dates.
- **Tracked items / All items:** Count of menu items configured to use daily production, including items without a batch. **Active (in stock) / In stock:** Count with remaining portions above zero. **Needs production:** Tracked items minus in-stock items; includes sold-out and no-batch items. These counts ignore search and do not check manual menu availability.
- **Produced / Cooked:** Portions recorded in the current batch after additions/corrections. **Remaining:** Prepared portions still recorded as available. Summary cards sum these quantities across all tracked items, regardless of the visible search/status filter; they mix portions of different dishes and are not ingredient weights.
- **Sold:** Produced minus Remaining, shown per item and in the PDF. Means net prepared portions consumed, not necessarily paid, completed or served portions. **Active:** Batch has remaining portions; **Sold out:** Batch exists with none left; **No batch:** No current production record.
- **Ingredients / availability:** Cooking deducts each recipe ingredient's per-portion quantity × added portions. Subsequent standalone-order consumption reduces prepared portions without deducting those ingredients again. A production-tracked menu item requires a positive branch balance and manual availability; raw stock alone does not make it sellable. Missing recipes allow production without ingredient deductions, so accurate recipe setup matters.
- **Order timing / platters:** Initial order lines normally consume production when preparation first starts; later-added lines consume immediately. Order acceptance checks availability but does not reserve initial portions. Platter components configured for daily production are skipped: they consume neither that item's standalone prepared balance nor its recipe ingredients through the platter deduction path.
- **Corrections / clearing:** Cook increases Produced and Remaining equally. Reduce normally decreases both and returns the removed portions' recipe ingredients to raw stock, preserving Sold; it is a recording correction, not physical uncooking or a wastage measure. Reduction below consumed portions is blocked. A reduction to zero clears the batch without ingredient return. Clear also removes the batch without ingredient return in this page; replacement, where recorded in movements, discards the old balance and deducts ingredients for the entire new batch.
- **Consumption limitations:** Initial preparation can still deduct cancelled lines, and lines added before initial preparation can be deducted twice. Order/line cancellation does not automatically restore prepared portions. Thus Sold and Remaining may require movement review before reconciliation with sales or physical stock.
- **Menu-item use (requested):** In the menu-item editor, configure the recipe quantities per portion, enable Uses daily production and retain manual Available if the item should be sellable. It then appears in Daily Productions; Quantity with + Cook records prepared portions, while later cooking adds to the existing batch. This setup does not itself create prepared stock.
- **Example (illustrative):** Qabuli uses 0.2 kg rice per portion. Cooking 30 portions deducts 6 kg rice and shows Produced 30, Remaining 30. Consumption of five standalone portions shows Produced 30, Remaining 25, Sold 5; no further rice deduction occurs for those portions. Cooking 10 more deducts 2 kg rice and shows Produced 40, Remaining 35, Sold 5. Sold 5 alone does not prove five paid sales.
- **PDF:** Current full branch snapshot of tracked items, production balances, net Sold, batch creator and last update; ignores the screen's search/status filter. Its generation date does not turn existing batch quantities into that day's activity. Prepared by is the batch creator, not necessarily everyone who subsequently cooked or adjusted it.

### Operations > Production Movements

- **Purpose / Total movements:** History explaining changes to prepared portions and who/what was recorded. Total movements counts matching events across all pages after search, movement-type and date filters; it counts neither portions nor orders. No date filter means available history, not just today.
- **When / dates:** When is the movement record's creation time. Start/end filters include both dates using Kabul calendar days, not order creation date or the batch's original cooking date.
- **Change / Balance:** Change = remaining portions after the event minus remaining before it; Balance shows those two historical values. Positive adds availability, negative removes it, zero can represent a notes-only adjustment. The after-balance is not necessarily today's balance or stock produced during the selected period.
- **Types:** Created starts a batch; Added increases it; Reduced corrects it downward; Adjusted records an absolute correction; Standalone order consumes portions; Restored returns portions up to the batch's produced quantity; Cleared removes the batch; Replaced swaps in a new batch and reports the net remaining change, not gross portions cooked.
- **Done by / Reference:** Recorded actor plus order number or notes. Consumption is attributed to the order creator, not necessarily the person starting kitchen work. Restored is a supported movement type, but current order cancellation does not automatically generate it.
- **Interpretation:** Negative changes include consumption, corrections, clearing and replacement; they are not all sales or wastage. Prepared-portion movements differ from raw-ingredient stock movements and contain no cash amount or production-cost measure. Date-filtered rows describe activity, not a reconstructed period-end stock balance.

### Operations > Discount Requests

- **Purpose / scope:** Pending queue and all-request history for manual percentage discounts on orders. Counts are requests, not distinct orders; duplicate pending requests can exist. The Pending badge counts the loaded page only; pagination count covers all matching requests.
- **Dates / status:** Pending queue has no date filter and includes pending only. All Requests filters by request creation date, inclusively in Kabul time, not order or decision date; search/status filters narrow rows and pagination count. Pending has not applied a discount; Approved writes the requested order percentage; Rejected leaves the order charge unchanged. Reviewer/time fields also record rejections.
- **Original total:** Current sum of all order-line subtotals, including cancelled lines, plus any linked reservation's duration/rate total even if Free, plus delivery fee for a delivery order; rounded to two decimals. It is recalculated, not an immutable amount captured when requested.
- **Discount / Final total:** Discount is the requested percentage; Final = Original × (1 − percentage/100), rounded to two decimals. Their difference is a previewed reduction, not a recorded refund or expense. Historical request previews can change with order/reservation changes, including rejected requests.
- **Charge mismatch:** These previews can exceed actual order charges because ordinary order totals exclude cancelled lines and free reservation fees. Approval applies the percentage to the ordinary order total calculation; displayed Final is not guaranteed to equal the resulting charge. The intended reconciliation of these differing preview rules is unresolved.
- **Limits / replacement:** Requests must be above 0% and at most 100%. Restaurant manager/admin limits determine approval authority: within manager limit, Manager/Admin/BranchAdmin; above it through admin limit, Admin/BranchAdmin only. Above both configured limits neither approval nor rejection currently succeeds; the manager-limit check takes precedence if the limits are misordered. Approval replaces the order percentage rather than adding percentages; a later pending request can overwrite an existing discount.
- **Paid-order limitation:** A previously pending request can still be approved on a completed order, changing historical order/revenue totals without a refund or payment-metadata change. Delivered/cancelled orders block approval. Approval is therefore not evidence that a customer received a cash refund.

### Operations > Discount Cards

- **Purpose / period:** Reusable percentage-discount entitlements for named customers in the branch, with validity dates and application limits. List and usage history have no period filter.
- **Total cards / Active:** Total is the current card count; Active counts stored active status only. It does not test expiry, start date, usage exhaustion or order minimum, so Active is not a count of cards currently usable.
- **Discount / Total discount:** Card percentage, including the detail sidebar labelled Total discount; not accumulated AFN savings. Successful application writes this percentage to the order's shared charge calculation immediately, without a manual approval request.
- **Usage / Usage stats:** Successful applications so far versus the usage limit; blank limit means unlimited. The count increments at application, before collection/completion, and cancellation does not return a use. It is not a paid-order count or a selected-period measure. A zero limit is displayed as unlimited but actually permits no uses.
- **Validity / eligibility:** Application requires active status, exact registered phone match, same restaurant/branch, unexhausted limit and an order total at least the minimum. The minimum compares the current full order total before application, including applicable extra charges. Expiry is checked against UTC Today and permits the expiry date itself; the displayed Valid from date is currently not enforced.
- **Order restrictions / history:** Completed/delivered/cancelled orders and orders already carrying a positive discount reject card application. Orders used lists currently linked orders of every status by order creation date, with their current discounted totals and order percentages; it is not an application-time or collections ledger and may differ from Used count after record/link changes.
- **Historical effect:** Editing a card changes future eligibility/discounts, not percentages already copied to orders. Removing a card does not remove those order percentages. No automatic card-use reversal or refund is established.

### Restaurant > Menu

- **Purpose / scope:** Current menu catalogue, pricing, preparation routing and selling availability, not sales performance. Includes selected-branch and applicable restaurant-wide legacy entries within loaded categories; uncategorized entries are not included in the category-based totals.
- **Categories / Menu Items / Platters:** Categories counts loaded categories; Menu Items counts dishes plus platters, unlike the dashboard's dish-only count. Platters is the subset of platter entries. Category badges also count dishes plus platters, not portions or sales.
- **Active / Hidden / Production:** Active counts currently available dishes/platters; Hidden counts manual availability switched off; Production counts dishes configured for daily production, regardless of whether portions exist. These are whole-catalogue counts before category, search, station and availability filters. Active plus Hidden need not equal Menu Items because visible items may be out of stock.
- **Availability labels:** Available = final selling availability; Hidden = manually unavailable; Sold out = a production-tracked dish with no remaining production, including no batch; Out of Stock = unavailable stock flag; Unavailable = other unavailable cases. Hidden does not guarantee removal from the public menu, which can still show unavailable entries.
- **Ordinary dishes / recipes:** Recipe quantities are ingredient amounts per portion, in each ingredient's stock unit. Recalculated availability requires enough of every recipe ingredient for one portion plus manual availability; a missing recipe makes an ordinary item unavailable. This is separate from low-stock thresholds and does not promise enough stock for every requested quantity. Daily-production availability follows Operations > Daily Productions.
- **Platters:** One sellable entry with its own selling price, station and component dish quantities, which can be fractional. Recalculated availability requires its component dishes to be available plus the platter's manual availability; it does not verify the multiplied ingredient demand for a full platter/order. Production-tracked component deductions follow the exception already documented under Daily Productions.
- **Price / cost / Profit:** Price is the current selling price. Item-detail Total cost = sum of per-portion recipe quantities × current ingredient unit costs; Profit = selling price minus that recipe cost. Platter cost sums component dish costs × included quantities, and its displayed Profit subtracts this from the platter price. These are current ingredient-cost estimates, excluding discounts, wages, overhead and other expenses; they are not realized profit or historical cost of sales. Missing recipes/costs can understate cost. Catalogue prices are rounded to whole AFN; detail calculations can show decimals.
- **Category order / filtered view:** Rank and item order control presentation, not popularity or priority of kitchen work. Search/category/station/status filters narrow displayed entries without changing catalogue KPI definitions.

### Restaurant > Kitchen Stations

- **Purpose:** Separates preparation responsibility, such as Main Kitchen, Grill and Juice Bar, by routing menu-item/platter order lines to assigned kitchen staff. A station is not a separate inventory balance, supplier account or sales total.
- **Routing / example:** A burger assigned to Grill and juice assigned to Juice Bar can belong to the same order. Grill staff handle the burger line; Juice Bar staff handle the juice line. The order remains in progress while another station has unfinished lines and becomes Ready when all non-cancelled lines are ready. A platter routes by its own station, not separately by each component's station.
- **Default / Custom:** Default Main Kitchen provides the fallback for entries without an assigned station; an active branch default takes precedence over a restaurant-wide default. Custom identifies another configured station; it does not measure workload or performance.
- **Active / Inactive:** Configuration status, separate from menu-item availability. Kitchen staff use their active station assignments; staff with no active assignments currently handle all stations. Inactivating a station alone therefore does not reliably stop orders or make related dishes unavailable.
- **Branch / filters:** Branch-specific stations apply to their branch; branchless stations apply restaurant-wide. Search and branch filters narrow the already loaded stations, not access scope. The Assigned Branch caption can incorrectly show All branches for a branch-specific station; its intended display needs verification.

### Inventory > Dashboard

- **Purpose / period:** Current raw-ingredient stock position with recent consumption/waste indicators. Current-stock KPIs have no period filter; consumption/waste use movement creation times within the preceding rolling 30 days, not order/payment dates. Embedded stock lists retain their own rules below.
- **Total Ingredients / Low Stock / Out of Stock:** Counts active ingredient records in scope. Low Stock means available quantity ≤ configured threshold; Out of Stock means exactly zero. These counts can overlap and must not be added. Embedded ingredient and alert lists include inactive records, so their counts can differ.
- **Inventory Value:** Sum of active ingredients' current quantity × current cost per stock unit. Purchase posting replaces unit cost with the received line's price, valuing existing stock at that new cost as well; this is neither weighted-average/FIFO valuation, purchase spending nor historical cost of sales. Ingredient cost is stored to two decimals, while invoice unit prices can have four, creating rounding differences.
- **Top Consumed Ingredients:** Up to five ingredient-name/unit groups ranked by greatest negative net order-movement quantity. Excludes cooking/production, waste, adjustments and transfers, and does not require completed/paid orders; it is not total ingredient usage. Ingredients can remain in the history after becoming inactive.
- **Consumption pie / percentages:** Uses absolute quantities of those five groups; converts kg to g and litres to ml before drawing shares. Percentages are shares of the displayed numeric quantities only, not all consumption or spending. Ranking occurs before conversion, and combining mass, volume and pieces prevents a meaningful common physical-unit comparison.
- **High Waste Ingredients:** Up to five name/unit groups with the lowest signed sum of waste movements, displayed as absolute amounts in their own units; positive corrections can offset negative waste. The badge is the displayed group count, at most five. This is recorded net quantity, not monetary loss or a waste percentage.
- **Stock transfers panel:** Shows up to five recent transfers where available. Pending is a request; approval moves quantity out of the source and into the destination; Rejected/Cancelled do not move it. Not shown to Branch Admins. These movements are not purchases or sales and are excluded from the Stock Movements list below.

### Inventory > Ingredients

- **Purpose:** Ingredient definitions, current quantities, units, replenishment thresholds, costs and recipe use. Lists include active and inactive records; search matches names and pagination count covers matching records, not stock volume. New ingredient definitions start with zero quantity and cost, not an automatic purchase.
- **Quantity / Cost per unit:** Current recorded raw-stock balance and AFN cost per recorded unit (kg, g, l, ml or pcs). Quantities of different units must not be added as one physical total. Cost reflects the current stored value, normally overwritten by purchase receipts/corrections, not an average purchase cost.
- **Minimum threshold / Status:** Configured replenishment warning point in that same unit. Low Stock means quantity ≤ threshold; otherwise OK. It is not a computed demand forecast, reorder quantity or proof that recipes cannot be prepared.
- **Used In:** Number of linked recipe entries, effectively distinct menu items using the ingredient; not sold portions, platter count or recent consumption.
- **Purchase history:** All non-draft invoice lines for that ingredient in scope, including supplierless purchases, ordered by purchase date. Quantity is received stock-unit quantity; Unit cost is that line's historical unit price; Total cost is the line charge. This history is not supplier payments or the complete stock-movement history.
- **Inventory PDFs:** Full inventory and out-of-stock downloads are current full-scope snapshots, ignoring screen search/page and including inactive ingredients. Row/total value = quantity × current unit cost. Their generation date does not make those balances activity for that date; the Low Stock PDF has the narrower rule documented under Low Stock Alerts.

### Inventory > Stock Movements

- **Purpose / exclusions:** Lists purchase, adjustment and waste movements only. Even All Types excludes order consumption, production deductions/returns and transfers; it cannot alone reconcile every change in stock.
- **Dates / count:** Defaults to movements created within the preceding rolling 30 days. Supplying either From or To replaces that default with the supplied inclusive Kabul-date bounds; an omitted opposite bound stays open. A specific-movement view bypasses the default lookback. Search/type/date filters narrow the pagination count, which counts events, not units or AFN.
- **Quantity / Total / Unit cost:** Quantity is the signed stock change: positive increases stock, negative reduces it. Purchases show historical movement quantity × recorded unit cost as Total, not payment. g/ml quantities are displayed as kg/l, while the unit cost remains per original stock unit; the total uses matching original units. Missing cost is not evidence of a zero-value purchase.
- **Corrections:** A revised movement changes current stock by revised effect minus old effect, not by adding the full revised quantity again. Purchase corrections also change current ingredient cost and, where linked, invoice-line quantity/price and invoice totals/status. Existing payment records are retained; reducing charges below payments can create reconciliation differences. Order movements are not editable here; purchase types remain purchases, while adjustment/waste can switch between those types.

### Inventory > Stock Levels

- **Purpose:** Uses the same current-stock list and definitions as Ingredients: Quantity, Minimum threshold, Cost per unit, Used In and Low Stock/OK. Includes inactive records and has no historical date cutoff.
- **Interpretation:** A current balance, not purchases or consumption in a period. Raw ingredients differ from prepared portions in Daily Productions; a low-stock warning is not the same as a menu item's one-portion availability check.

### Inventory > Stock Adjustments

- **Purpose:** Current ingredient balances with recording corrections classified as Adjustment or Waste. The Adjustment Queue panel is explanatory, not a pending-approval count or a separate list of outstanding corrections.
- **Quantity / effect:** Quantity is a signed delta in the ingredient's stock unit, not a target balance: new balance = current balance + delta. A negative resulting balance and zero change are rejected. Corrections change quantity and availability, not supplier debt, payment history or a separately recorded expense.
- **Waste distinction:** New waste entries use the entered sign; selecting Waste does not itself force a reduction. Editing an existing waste movement forces its quantity negative. Positive/negative waste entries can therefore offset in the dashboard's net waste figures; a Waste label alone does not prove gross physical loss.
- **History:** View Adjustments opens Stock Movements filtered to adjustment only; waste has a separate type and the same default lookback/date rules apply. Ingredient balance columns retain the Ingredients definitions.

### Inventory > Low Stock Alerts

- **Purpose / count:** Current ingredients with quantity ≤ minimum threshold, including inactive records and zero stock. Item count is matching ingredients, not units, order shortages or outstanding purchases; no activity-period filter.
- **Available / Minimum / Status:** Current balance and configured threshold in each ingredient's unit. Exactly zero is labelled Out of Stock; other matching quantities are labelled Low Stock. The alert is a replenishment signal, not an automatic purchase, forecast or guaranteed recipe shortage.
- **PDF difference:** The Low Stock PDF includes only positive quantities at/below threshold; zero-stock ingredients belong in the separate Out of Stock PDF. Thus its count can differ from this alert list and the active-only dashboard KPI.

### Procurement > Shared Scope and Financial Meaning

- **Coverage:** Invoice/payment lists include all matching records, newest purchase/payment date first; table pagination does not limit coverage. Searches include all matches. Supplier totals/ledgers cover all related history, independently of invoice/payment search filters. Normal branch scope applies.
- **Period / headers:** No workspace-wide date-range control. Month spend and Payables in page headers repeat the dashboard definitions below; do not add repeated totals. Invoice/payment searches can change totals derived from those lists, without narrowing supplier history. Today/current-month comparisons and form date defaults use the UTC date, which can still be yesterday during early Kabul morning.
- **Distinctions:** Purchases measure invoice charges; supplier payments record disbursements; remaining balances measure current liabilities after recorded payments. Purchases are not automatically expenses, payments are not additional purchase charges, and none of these measures establishes restaurant profit.

### Procurement > Dashboard

- **Total purchases / Month spend:** Sum of non-draft loaded invoice totals whose purchase date is in the current UTC calendar month, including same-month future-dated invoices. It is not strictly month-to-date or based on payment dates; includes supplierless purchases.
- **Today's purchases:** Sum of non-draft loaded invoice totals whose purchase date equals UTC Today; not invoices created, received or paid today unless those dates coincide.
- **Pending invoices:** Number of loaded invoices with Unpaid or Partially Paid status, including supplierless invoices and possible zero-balance/zero-value Unpaid invoices. Not draft approvals or necessarily overdue invoices.
- **Supplier balance / Payables:** Sum of all listed suppliers' current outstanding balances, active and inactive; excludes supplierless invoice balances. This is an all-history balance, not this month's unpaid purchases.
- **Payments made:** Sum of all matching supplier-payment amounts, with no month/today restriction. Excludes supplierless initial amounts stored directly on invoices; search can narrow the included payments. Not comparable to monthly purchases as if both covered the same period.
- **Monthly purchasing chart:** Actually sums non-draft loaded invoices by purchase date and shows the latest seven dates with purchases, newest first; not seven calendar days or monthly buckets. Missing purchase dates are omitted.
- **Top suppliers / Spend by supplier:** Five suppliers ranked by all-history non-draft purchase charges; Purchased and Balance use the supplier definitions below. Spend here means invoice value, not money paid; inactive suppliers can be included. Chart bars scale to the largest shown value with a minimum visible width, not a spending percentage.
- **Recent / Upcoming panels:** Recent invoices shows up to six by purchase date/creation time, including drafts; Upcoming supplier payments shows up to six Unpaid/Partially Paid invoices in that same order, not scheduled payments or due-date order. Recent payment activity shows up to five by payment date/creation time. Decorative trend arrows are not calculated growth rates.

### Procurement > Purchase Invoices

- **Purpose / dates:** Supplier and supplierless ingredient purchase charges, stock receipt state and current settlement. Invoice Date is purchase date; Due date is optional expected payment timing; neither is payment date. List search/status filters include all matching invoices; no purchase-date filter is exposed on this page.
- **Line quantity / Unit price / Line total:** Quantity in the ingredient's recorded stock unit × historical line price per that unit, rounded to two decimals for each line total. Invoice total sums those rounded line totals. No separate invoice tax, discount, freight or credit adjustment is added.
- **Paid / Remaining:** For supplier invoices, Paid is the sum of linked payment records, capped at the current invoice total; Remaining = max(Total − Paid, 0). Supplierless invoices instead use their directly stored paid amount. These are current cumulative amounts across all payment dates, not paid/outstanding as of the purchase date.
- **Draft / stock receipt:** Draft retains line charges but has zero recorded Paid, no received stock and is excluded from purchase/supplier totals. Its displayed Remaining is not an established payable. Approval posts stock once and makes it non-draft; stock receipt does not require payment, and later payment does not receive stock again.
- **Unpaid / Partially Paid / Paid:** No positive recorded payment; positive payment below total; or positive payment covering total. A zero-value invoice can remain Unpaid despite zero Remaining. These are financial states, not proof of bank settlement or an overdue classification.
- **Cash / No Supplier:** Means no supplier association, not automatically paid. A non-draft cash invoice can have a positive Remaining and appear among unpaid invoices, but contributes nothing to supplier balances and has no supplier-payment entries. How a later supplierless balance is settled through the current screens remains unresolved.
- **Corrections / evidence:** Linked purchase-movement corrections can recalculate invoice charges/status while retaining payments, so zero Remaining may hide payment amounts above the revised charge. Attachments and audit history provide evidence; uploading/removing an attachment does not change stock, charges or payments.

### Procurement > Create Purchase Invoice

- **Purpose / preview:** Estimates ingredient purchase charges and initial settlement; Invoice total sums displayed line totals, Amount paid is the proposed initial payment, Remaining = max(preview total − initial payment, 0). These are proposed values until recorded; final totals are recalculated from quantities/prices and can differ through rounding.
- **Purchasing units:** Ingredients recorded in g/ml are entered as kg/l: quantity × 1,000 and unit price ÷ 1,000, preserving line value. Other units remain unchanged. Saved invoice details show the original stock units, so quantities/prices can look different without changing the purchase value.
- **Receipt versus draft:** A non-draft invoice receives its stock and replaces current ingredient unit costs; an unpaid supplier purchase still increases stock. A draft does neither. Quantities must be positive and unit prices nonnegative; zero-cost lines are possible and do not establish a payment.
- **Initial payment:** On a non-draft supplier invoice, a positive initial amount creates a supplier payment dated the purchase date. With no supplier it is stored on the invoice directly, absent from Payments made. Initial amount cannot exceed total. A draft's entered initial amount is not retained as a payment and does not become a payment on approval.
- **Dates / payment method:** Purchase date determines purchase-period inclusion; due date does not alter totals or automatically schedule payment. Method describes the recorded initial payment, not a bank/card execution. The page's shared header totals describe existing records, not the unsaved invoice.

### Procurement > Suppliers

- **Purpose:** Supplier identity, current activity status and cumulative purchase/payment relationship; no date filter. Active/Inactive is trading configuration, not whether a balance is settled. Inactive suppliers remain in financial totals; new invoices require an active supplier, while existing debt can still receive payments.
- **Purchases / Total purchases:** Sum of all related non-draft invoice totals, regardless of purchase/payment dates or current payment status. Excludes drafts and supplierless purchases.
- **Paid / Total paid / Balance:** Total paid sums all supplier-payment records; Balance = max(Total purchases − Total paid, 0). Not period payments or a credit-wallet balance. After invoice corrections, total payment records may exceed the invoice's capped Paid; supplier net balance can differ from summed positive invoice balances.
- **Ledger debit / credit / running balance:** Non-draft invoices add debits on purchase dates; payments add credits on payment dates. Running balance starts at zero and accumulates debit minus credit, sorted by date with invoices before payments on the same date; it is not capped at zero. Backdated payments can produce negative intermediate balances. Current header Balance still has a zero floor; no opening balance or independent supplier credit is included.

### Procurement > Payments

- **Purpose / Amount / Date:** Recorded supplier disbursements against individual non-draft invoices. Amount is that payment, not invoice total or cumulative Paid; Date is the recorded payment date, separate from record creation and purchase dates. Method/reference identify the recorded transaction and do not verify an external transfer. List includes all search matches but has no date-range control.
- **Payment limits / effect:** A positive payment cannot exceed the invoice's current Remaining and reduces that balance; supplier/invoice/branch must match. Draft invoices cannot receive payments. This does not reduce stock or add another purchase/expense, and there is no general payment-refund/reversal screen.
- **Voucher figures:** Paid Before sums this invoice's earlier payments in payment-date, creation-time, then record order; Current payment is this record; voucher Total Paid adds the two; Remaining = max(current invoice total − that cumulative amount, 0). It excludes later payments, unlike the invoice's current balance. Voucher Status is the invoice's current status; its Paid mark uses the reconstructed Remaining.
- **Historical limitation:** Voucher balances are recalculated from current invoice charges and the currently dated payment history, not immutable snapshots. Purchase corrections/backdated payments can change old voucher balances. Reprinting/downloading is not another payment; supplierless invoice-paid amounts do not produce these supplier vouchers.

### Procurement > Payables

- **Purpose / Outstanding balance:** Current supplier net debt, using Dashboard Supplier balance and Suppliers Balance definitions; all history rather than a selected period or balance as of a past date.
- **Unpaid invoices / table:** Count and list of all matching Unpaid/Partially Paid invoices. Includes supplierless and possible zero-balance invoices; excludes drafts and Paid status. It can therefore disagree with the supplier-only Outstanding balance.
- **Suppliers with balance:** Number of suppliers with positive current Balance, including inactive suppliers; supplier balance list sorts largest first. Supplier totals are not limited to the visible unpaid-invoice set.
- **Interpretation:** Payables includes debt regardless of due date; no overdue aging or payment schedule is calculated here. Compare supplier net balances with invoice balances and retained payments before assuming they reconcile, especially after purchase corrections. Supplierless settlement and handling of payments above revised invoice charges remain unresolved.

### HR > Staff

- **Purpose / scope:** Current employee identities, roles, contact details, assigned shift, employment status and salary profiles; not a headcount history or wage-payment report. Normally includes branch-assigned staff plus restaurant-wide administrator profiles; Branch Admin visibility has additional administrator-account restrictions. Includes the full matching roster, without an employment-date filter.
- **Search / role:** Search matches name or stored role, not email, phone or the custom title shown for Other. Kitchen-station responsibility is separate from the employee's working shift; station meaning is defined under Restaurant > Kitchen Stations.
- **Employment status:** Active, Inactive and Resigned are distinct stored statuses; only Active is eligible for payroll generation. The table currently labels every non-Inactive row Active, so Resigned staff can appear active. Inactivation does not erase existing payroll or debt; employee deletion is not archival and can remove linked HR/payroll history.
- **Salary / profile:** Salary is the agreed base amount or rate in AFN, labelled monthly, weekly, daily or hourly; not gross earnings, take-home pay or money paid. Allowances and deductions are fixed amounts copied into each generated payroll, not percentages; overtime rate is AFN per overtime hour. Payment Day is a configured day-of-month, not evidence of payment or an automatic transfer schedule. Payroll Active is separate from employment status.
- **Linked salary-profile summaries:** Total Earnings sums payroll expense amounts defined under Payroll Records, including drafts; Deductions sums ordinary plus advance deductions across all payrolls. Total Paid sums salary-payment records; Advances sums all issued advances, applied and open. These cover the employee's complete history across branches, not selected-branch or selected-period totals. Earnings already subtracts ordinary deductions, so subtracting Deductions again double-counts them.

### HR > Attendance

- **Purpose / date:** Daily Present, Absent or Leave selections for staff currently assigned to the selected shift. Today uses the browser's local calendar date; no historical-date selector. Assigned staff includes inactive/resigned staff still linked to that shift, not only payroll-eligible employees.
- **Assigned Staff / Present / Absent / Leave:** Assigned Staff counts current shift members; the other cards count their current selections. Unmarked staff default to Present, and changing a selection changes these counts before it is saved. They therefore do not prove attendance was recorded.
- **Saved attendance:** One status per employee, date and shift; a correction updates the existing matching record rather than intentionally adding another. The same employee can have separate saved records for different shifts on one date. Removing/changing a current assignment does not rewrite old attendance.
- **Interpretation / exclusions:** Present is a recorded status, not measured hours or proof of clock-in/out; Absent/Leave do not automatically deduct salary. Leave is not an approved leave request, entitlement balance or a paid/unpaid-leave calculation. Saved monthly attendance and its percentage appear on Shifts, with a different denominator from the main dashboard's Attendance Today gauge.

### HR > Shifts

- **Purpose / summary:** Recurring named working-hour groups and their current staff assignments, plus saved monthly attendance. Shifts counts shift definitions; Assigned Staff counts distinct linked employees across those shifts, irrespective of employment status. Neither counts employees actually present today.
- **Shift cards:** Start/End are configured working times, not clocked attendance. Duration is end minus start, treating an earlier end as next day; equal times show zero hours. Per-card Assigned Staff counts all members; five names are displayed and +N represents additional members. A shift does not generate payable hours, overtime, rota dates or salary by itself.
- **Assignment history:** Each employee has one current shift assignment. Removing a shift clears current assignments and the shift reference on saved attendance, but retains the attendance records/statuses; historical shift filtering can consequently lose that association.
- **Monthly attendance / dates:** Grid shows the browser's current calendar month, with no month/date-range selector. Available history starts at the server-calendar month's first day; different browser/server dates near a month boundary can omit records. Future-dated records in the displayed month are not excluded.
- **Filters / population:** Employee-name and shift filters narrow both the grid and its summary. Only employees with matching saved records appear; staff without records, unrecorded days and future blank cells do not become absences or enter the percentage denominator. Shift choices come from shifts present in that month's saved records.
- **Present / Absent / Leave / %:** Status cards count matching saved shift records. Overall and per-employee rate = Present ÷ (Present + Absent + Leave) × 100, rounded to whole percent; zero with no tracked records. Leave lowers the rate. Per-employee colour is green at ≥80%, amber at 60–79%, otherwise red; this is a display threshold, not a payroll penalty.
- **Multiple shifts limitation:** All matching shift records enter counts/rates, but a grid cell displays only one record per employee/date. Without a specific shift filter, multiple same-day shift statuses can therefore be hidden behind one mark; percentages are record-based, not unique-day attendance rates.

### Payroll > Dashboard

- **Payroll This Month / This Year:** Sum of payroll expense amounts for Approved/Paid records with period end on/after the current Kabul month/year's first day. Drafts are excluded. Includes drafts and all matching periods, not just this month; subsequent role changes can reclassify historical amounts. Chart widths scale to the largest bar with a minimum visible width, not a percentage share.

### Payroll > Payroll Wizard

- **Purpose / eligibility:** Calculates Draft payroll from current salary profiles for Active, Payroll Active employees assigned to the active branch. Empty employee selection means all eligible employees, not none. The displayed selection list/count can include inactive/resigned payroll-enabled profiles that generation excludes.
- **Run Type / dates:** Monthly or Weekly labels the run; the employee's Salary Type independently determines the base calculation. Inclusive start/end dates can be equal and need not be a full calendar month/week; monthly/weekly base is not automatically prorated for shorter or longer periods, hire date, absence or leave.
- **Base calculation:** Monthly/weekly employees receive their full configured base once. Daily base = daily rate × entered Regular Days, defaulting to inclusive calendar days in the period, including weekends. Hourly base = hourly rate × entered Regular Hours, defaulting to zero. Fractional days/hours are allowed; attendance and shift duration supply neither automatically.
- **Adjustments:** The same entered bonus and overtime hours apply to every included employee; overtime uses each employee's own rate. Profile allowances/deductions apply once per record, independently of days/hours. Salary calculation and advance recovery use the definitions below; there is no automatic tax or attendance-penalty calculation.
- **Generated count / regeneration:** Counts created or recalculated records, excluding Paid records and any record already having a payment. Unpaid records for the exact employee/start/end can be recalculated and reset from Approved to Draft, removing approval recognition until approved again. Generated records can therefore be recalculations, not additional payroll debt.
- **Period overlap / branch limitation:** Exact employee/start/end identifies one payroll across run types and branches; an unpaid regeneration can change its run type/branch. Different overlapping date pairs are not generally prevented, so successful generation is not proof that earnings were not counted twice.

### Payroll > Payroll Records

- **Purpose / filters:** Employee-period salary calculations and current settlement. Search matches employee name, stored role or payroll notes; status and Monthly/Weekly filters narrow the list. Period type is run type, not salary basis; no date-range control is exposed. Where period-range filtering is used, inclusion is inclusive overlap, not full containment or prorating.
- **Gross / earnings:** Gross = Base + Allowances + Bonuses + (Overtime Hours × Overtime Rate). Overtime amount, gross and net are rounded to two decimals. Base is the period amount calculated by the wizard, not necessarily the employee's unchanged profile rate.
- **Deductions / Net:** Ordinary deductions and linked salary-advance deductions are separate. Net = max(Gross − Ordinary Deductions − Advance Deductions, 0); it is salary payable after advance recovery, not money already received.
- **Payroll expense:** max(Gross − Ordinary Deductions, 0), before subtracting advances; used in the dashboard only for Approved/Paid records. Advance recovery reduces the later payment obligation, not earned payroll cost. Approval contributes this cost to financial reporting; it does not create a separate ordinary-expense entry.
- **Paid / Balance:** Paid is cumulative linked salary payments, capped at net salary; Balance/Outstanding = max(Net − Paid, 0). These are current all-payment-date balances, not balances as of period end or the displayed payment date. They exclude advances already deducted from Net.
- **Statuses:** Draft is an unapproved calculation, excluded from dashboard cost/outstanding and ineligible for payments. Approved is recognized payroll, possibly unpaid, partly paid or zero-net. Paid requires a positive net salary fully covered by recorded salary payments; zero-net records can remain Approved. Only Admin/Branch Admin can approve, even if Finance Manager sees an approval control.
- **Detail / historical meaning:** Salary Calculation separates earnings from ordinary/advance deductions; Payable Summary repeats Gross, Net, Paid and Outstanding. Payment and Applied Advance tables are the linked records, not extra charges/deductions to add again; audit history is recorded changes, not extra payroll. Profile changes do not automatically recalculate existing amounts, while displayed employee role/salary-type labels use the current profile.

### Payroll > Salary Advances

- **Open / Applied:** Open means not linked to payroll; Applied means linked, including to Draft/unpaid payroll, not confirmed cash recovery or employee repayment. Later-dated advances remain open; ordinary profile deductions are separate.

No salary-based limit or partial-advance carry-forward is calculated when advances exceed available earnings; net floors at zero. Treatment/recovery of that excess remains unresolved. ### Payroll > Payroll Payments

Quantity is not ingredient stock or automatically measured work hours. No separate tax, discount, retention, freight or contract-value adjustment is added.
- **Paid / Balance / Remaining:** Paid sums linked payment records, capped at current invoice Total; Remaining = max(Total − Paid, 0). These include payments across all recorded dates, not payment activity in the invoice-date period or the balance as it stood then.
- **Draft / Approved:** Draft retains line charges and may display a positive Balance, but Paid is zero, payments are prohibited, and it is excluded from service-cost, contractor-ledger and recognized-payables totals. Approved means posted and unpaid; approval recognizes the charge, not a cash transfer. A zero-total unpaid invoice can remain Approved and count as Open.
- **Partially Paid / Paid:** Positive payments below Total yield Partially Paid; payments covering Total yield Paid. These concern settlement, not service-contract completion or independent verification that work was performed. A change in agreement status does not change invoice status.
- **References / detail:** Blank invoice numbers display as CINV-number references; those generated labels are not necessarily searchable as stored invoice numbers. Detail repeats Total, Paid and Remaining and shows service lines, full linked payment history, supporting attachments and audit changes. Attachments/audit entries are evidence/context, not additional charges or payments; there is no general invoice-level edit, void or refund screen.

### Contractors > Create Invoice

- **Purpose:** Establishes a Draft calculation or an Approved service charge, optionally with an initial payment; not an ingredient purchase. Approved is the default. A linked contract must belong to the selected provider/branch, but can have any status and does not supply the invoice amount or enforce its value/date limits. Invoices may have no linked contract.
- **Invoice Total preview:** Sums entered quantity × unit price without rounding each line first; the saved invoice instead sums rounded line totals. Fractional quantities can therefore produce a small preview/saved-total difference. Quantity must be positive and unit price nonnegative; blank line description falls back to the service type. Service Type categorizes work; it is not an inventory item or separate automatic charge.
- **Amount Paid Initially:** Blank/zero records no payment. A positive amount creates a real linked payment record dated on Invoice Date, using the chosen method/reference; it cannot exceed the saved Total and is forbidden for Draft. Partial/full initial settlement determines Partially Paid/Paid immediately. Initial payments are already included in payment history and Paid totals, not a second amount to add.
- **Negative amounts / evidence:** Negative initial amounts are ignored rather than creating refunds or credits; intended adjustment handling remains unresolved. A positive Remaining or a recorded payment does not verify an external transfer or establish compliance with the service agreement.

### Contractors > Payments

- **Purpose / dates:** Service-payment history, including payments recorded initially with invoices. Amount is one transaction, not the invoice total or cumulative Paid. Payment Date is separate from invoice, contract and creation dates; ordering is payment date then entry time, newest first. Search matches provider name, stored invoice number, reference or notes; no on-page date-range selector. Date ranges, where used, apply inclusively to payment date.
- **Limits / effect:** A payment must be positive, no greater than the invoice's current Remaining, linked to the correct provider/invoice and within the active branch. Drafts cannot receive payments; inactive providers' recognized invoices can. Partial/full payments reduce current debt and update invoice status, without creating another service expense or changing inventory.
- **Interpretation:** Method/reference record the declared transaction, not a bank integration. Future-dated payments affect current balances immediately rather than waiting until that date; displayed balances are not historical snapshots. No general posted-payment refund, void or reversal screen is supplied.

### Contractors > Payables

- **Purpose / Outstanding Balance:** Current all-history net service debt, using Dashboard Remaining Payments; not unpaid contract value, selected-period cost or only overdue invoices.
- **Open Invoices / table:** Count and list of matching Approved/Partially Paid invoices, including possible zero-balance Approved rows. Draft/Paid records are excluded. Invoice-list filters can narrow this count/table while the Outstanding Balance remains full-scope, so these figures need not describe the same population.
- **Interpretation:** Default order is latest invoice date, not earliest due date; no aging buckets or scheduled-payment calculation. Invoice Total is the recorded service charge and Balance is current unpaid amount. Old debts remain included, regardless of contract completion/expiry or provider inactivation.

### Finance > Expenses

- **Purpose / scope:** Records ordinary operating expenses for the selected branch, such as transport or incidental supplies. These are separate from ingredient purchase invoices, contractor service invoices and payroll. Recording the same cost in both places duplicates recognized expense; there is no automatic cross-module duplicate check or offset.
- **Total Expenses card:** Count of all current expense records in scope, not their monetary value. Includes all dates and currencies, regardless of table search, date or currency filters.
- **Total AFN card:** Sum of all expenses' saved AFN equivalents, including converted USD expenses; not only expenses originally entered in AFN. Full scoped history, independent of table filters.
- **Total USD card:** Sum of original USD amounts on USD expense records, across all scoped history. Those amounts are already converted into Total AFN; these two cards must not be added together.
- **This Month AFN card:** Sum of saved AFN equivalents whose expense date falls in the current UTC year/month. Not controlled by the table's From/To dates; includes future-dated entries within that month, rather than stopping at Today. Early on the first Kabul morning, the UTC month can still be the previous month.
- **Original Amount / Exchange Rate / AFN Equivalent:** AFN expenses retain their original amount. USD expenses use original amount × saved exchange rate, where the rate means AFN per USD; the saved AFN equivalent has two decimal places. This is a manually recorded rate, not a live currency feed or later revaluation. For example, USD 10 at AFN 70 per USD contributes AFN 700 to Total AFN and USD 10 to Total USD.
- **Dates / table coverage:** From/To inclusively filter expense date, not entry/change time; an omitted bound is open, and the default is no date restriction. Search matches name/description; currency filters the original currency. Default order is newest expense date. Table counts and pagination cover all matching records, not a newest-100 subset; cards still describe the unfiltered scope.
- **Recognition / limitations:** There is no Draft, Approved or Paid expense status and no separate expense-payment balance. A saved expense immediately contributes to operating cost on its expense date; Finance also treats it as an expense payment without independently verifying cash movement. Zero/negative amounts or rates are not prohibited and can reduce/invert totals; they are not automatically classified refunds. Intended adjustment/validation policy is unverified.

### Finance > Expense Detail, Voucher and History

- **Detail / voucher amounts:** Repeat the current expense's original amount, currency, saved conversion and expense date. Voucher number identifies that expense; printing/reprinting does not create another expense or payment. A voucher is generated from current values, not an immutable original after later edits; its printing timestamp is separate from expense date.
- **History meaning:** Created, Updated and Deleted entries are change events, not additional spending. Amounts describe the event's expense snapshot; an update records the new values, with changed fields identifying differences. Adding history-row amounts would count the same expense repeatedly.
- **History dates / filters:** From/To inclusively filter the change timestamp's Kabul date, not the expense's financial date. Search matches expense name/description; action filters distinguish creation, updates and deletion. Newest changes appear first; paginated counts refer to events. Deleting a live expense removes it from current totals while retaining its deletion-history entry, rather than recording a financial refund.

### Finance > Reports > Shared Period and Interpretation

- **Views / scope:** Reports contains Orders, Finance, Inventory, Staff Performance and Menu Items. Normal selected-branch scope applies; Branch Admins remain branch-restricted. Amounts are AFN unless explicitly identified otherwise. Counts, percentages, quantities and monetary values are distinct measures, even where a caption/format is misleading.
- **Applied period:** Defaults to Today on the browser's local calendar. Today is one date; Last 7/30 includes today and the preceding 6/29 dates; This Month runs from the first day through today. Pending date inputs/presets do not change displayed results until applied. Both applied bounds are inclusive; reversed/empty dates leave the previous valid report. Unlike the main Dashboard's sales chart, future report dates are not prohibited.
- **Date basis:** Timestamp ranges cover complete Asia/Kabul days; business date fields use the selected dates directly. Orders use creation, not completion/payment time. Invoice charges, payments, attendance and payroll have their specific date rules below. A common header period does not mean every card is restricted to it.
- **Current versus historical:** Reports recalculate from current records, statuses, recipes, profiles and balances. A past period is not a locked accounting snapshot or balance as of its end date. Current stock/payables/profile figures can include changes after that period. Data is not a live feed; reapplying identical dates does not necessarily refresh Orders, Finance, Inventory or Staff results.
- **Coverage / exports:** Headline aggregates cover their full eligible populations independently of operational workspace pagination. Some detail tables/lookups intentionally return only the recent/top records specified below; they are not full ledgers. PDFs regenerate data and can have extra sections, shorter tables or separate configuration, so are not exact screen copies. Rounding, differing date/status rules and partial tables can prevent apparent reconciliation.

### Finance > Reports > Orders: Cards

- **Successful sales basis:** Completed and Delivered orders created within the applied period, using Dashboard > Shared Sales Meaning: non-cancelled item value plus eligible reservation/delivery charges, less the order discount. Other order statuses do not contribute revenue; payment collection is not required.
- **Total Revenue:** Sum of those successful order values. The Lost subtitle instead sums current calculated values of Cancelled orders in the period; it is potential order value, not refunded money or an immutable original lost-sales value. Cancelled lines are excluded even from Lost, so a fully cancelled order can show no lost value.
- **Total Orders / Avg Value:** Total Orders counts every order status created in the period. Avg Value = successful revenue ÷ number of successful orders, or zero if none; its denominator is not Total Orders when unfinished/cancelled orders exist.
- **Completed / Cancelled:** Completed combines Completed and Delivered order counts, unlike an operations count restricted to Completed alone. Cancelled counts cancelled orders, not cancelled lines or units. Both use order creation dates and current status.
- **Food Revenue:** Total Revenue − Delivery Revenue − Reservation Revenue. Represents the discounted item portion of successful order value, not gross menu sales, profit or food-only dish categories.
- **Delivery Revenue:** Only delivery fees on successful delivery orders, after each order's percentage discount; not all revenue from delivery-type orders.
- **Reservation Revenue:** Discounted fee/prepaid reservation charges linked to successful orders; free reservations contribute zero. Uses the shared current table-rate/duration calculation, not reservation payments or standalone bookings. A reservation linked to multiple successful orders can contribute repeatedly; reservation cancellation status is not separately excluded.

### Finance > Reports > Orders: Charts and Tables

- **By Order Type:** Each type's count and revenue include successful orders only; revenue is their full discounted order value, including applicable charges. Bar width = that successful type count ÷ all-status Total Orders × 100. Bars need not total 100% when other statuses exist, and delivery-type revenue is not the Delivery Revenue fee card.
- **Order Status:** Counts all period orders by current status; categories together reconcile to all-status Total Orders. This measures current service outcomes of orders created in the period, not a timeline of status changes during it.
- **Top Selling Items:** Up to ten entries ranked by non-cancelled line quantity on successful orders. Quantity counts ordered units, with a platter counted as a sellable unit, not exploded components. Revenue = quantity × recorded order-time price, falling back to current menu/platter price for older lines; it is gross before discounts and excludes delivery/reservation charges. Grouping by product name can merge distinct products or a dish and platter with the same name. This is not the main Dashboard's all-status best-seller calculation.
- **Daily Breakdown:** Successful order count and discounted full-order revenue for dates with successful activity; no zero-sales dates are inserted. Inclusion uses Kabul date boundaries, but rows are currently grouped by UTC creation date. Early-Kabul orders can therefore appear under the preceding date, potentially outside the displayed calendar range.
- **Average Preparation Time:** Mean minutes from recorded preparation start to preparation end among successful period orders with both timestamps, rounded to two decimals. Excludes missing timings and does not measure queue time before preparation, customer waiting or delivery time.
- **Menu Item Sales Lookup:** Name search across English/Dari/Pashto product names, within the period and active branch. Units and revenue follow the Menu Items report's successful-sales rules, with net item revenue after attributed discount and platters separate. Only the first 200 matching selling products are returned; this lookup is not a complete unlimited export and does not change the Orders cards. Its net revenue differs from the gross Top Selling Items table.
- **Orders PDF summary / Peak Hours:** The PDF's Total Revenue (non-cancelled) still means Completed/Delivered revenue, not every non-cancelled status. Peak Hours shows the five busiest dated Kabul-hour buckets by all-status order creation count, including cancellations; it does not combine the same hour across different days or measure peak successful revenue.
- **Orders PDF performance tables:** Separate calculations from Staff Performance. Waiter Performance groups all-status period orders by the employee receiving them, without requiring a Waiter role; Delivery Boy Performance groups all-status delivery orders by assigned driver. Both show up to ten name groups, ranked by their displayed counts. Revenue is gross item value including cancelled lines, before discounts/charges. Counts can repeat one order for multiple joined item lines, so are not reliable distinct order/delivery counts; same-name employees can merge. These PDF tables must not be interpreted using the Staff report's creator/status/net-value rules.

### Finance > Reports > Finance: Profit and Cost Definitions

- **Total Revenue:** Same successful, creation-date order value as Orders Total Revenue, including discounted eligible delivery/reservation charges. Not confirmed receipts, unpaid-balance recovery or sales limited to menu products.
- **Cost of Goods Sold / COGS:** Sold non-cancelled units on successful period orders × current branch-effective recipe quantities × current ingredient unit costs. Platters expand their current component recipes/quantities. This is a recipe-cost estimate, not historical purchase-lot/FIFO cost or the sum of actual order-consumption movements. Missing recipes/costs understate it; later recipe/cost changes can change historical profit.
- **Wastage:** Sum of absolute quantities on Waste movements created in the period × each movement's recorded unit cost, falling back to current ingredient cost. Positive waste corrections are also counted as cost rather than offsets. Production losses/adjustments are not automatically wastage unless recorded as Waste.
- **Daily / Operational Expenses:** Saved AFN equivalents of live ordinary expense records whose expense date is in the period. Includes USD conversions; excludes deleted expenses and separate contractor/payroll/inventory purchase records.
- **Contractor Expenses:** Total charges of non-Draft service invoices dated in the period, whether unpaid, partially paid or paid. Contract value/status and payment dates do not define cost inclusion; these invoices do not also create ordinary expense entries.
- **Payroll Cost:** Sum of max(gross salary − ordinary deductions, 0) for Approved/Paid payrolls approved in the period. If approval time is missing, salary-period end date determines inclusion instead. Salary-advance recovery does not reduce this earned cost. Not a salary-period overlap calculation; the PDF's Monthly Payroll Cost label still means the selected approval-date period, not necessarily one month.
- **Total Expenses:** COGS + Wastage + Daily Expenses + Contractor Expenses + Payroll Cost. Inventory purchase value, debt balances, supplier/contractor/payroll payments and new salary advances are not extra profit deductions. A cost manually duplicated in ordinary Expenses is nevertheless counted again.
- **Gross Profit card:** Revenue − COGS; before wastage, ordinary expenses, contractor costs and payroll. **Net Profit card:** Revenue − Total Expenses; may be negative. **Profit Margin card:** Net Profit ÷ Revenue × 100, rounded to two decimals; displays zero when Revenue is zero/nonpositive. These are estimates from recorded data, not audited after-tax profit or cash in the bank; no additional tax/depreciation calculation is supplied.

### Finance > Reports > Finance: Purchase, Contractor and Payroll Cards

- **Purchase Value:** Non-Draft purchase invoice totals whose purchase date is in the period, plus purchase movements created in the period that have no linked invoice line. Unlinked movements contribute signed quantity × recorded cost/current-cost fallback. Includes Cash / No Supplier purchases. This acquires inventory rather than immediately increasing profit expenses.
- **Supplier Payments:** Sum of supplier-payment transactions dated in the period, including initial payments linked to supplier invoices. Not the paid-to-date total on period purchases. Cash / No Supplier direct settlement is not a supplier-payment transaction and is omitted from this card.
- **Supplier Payables:** Sum of current remaining balances on all-history non-Draft supplier-linked purchase invoices, not just period purchases. Excludes supplierless invoices and is not an overdue-only figure or an end-of-period historical balance. Future-dated records/payments can already affect current debt; this invoice-balance sum can differ from a globally netted supplier dashboard balance.
- **Contractor Expenses / Payments / Payables:** Expenses follows the service-cost definition above. Payments sums contractor-payment transactions dated in the period. Payables sums current remaining balances on all-history non-Draft service invoices, independently of selected dates or agreement status; not unbilled contract value or only overdue debt. Do not add payments/payables to service expense.
- **Payroll Cost / Payroll Payments:** Cost uses the approval-date cohort defined above. Payments sums salary-payment transactions dated in the period, even if the linked salary period/approval lies elsewhere; advances are separate. Cost and payments therefore need not match.
- **Outstanding Salaries:** Sum of current remaining net salary on all-history Approved payrolls, irrespective of selected dates. Excludes Draft and Paid payrolls. This is neither period payroll cost nor the narrower overlapping-period debt in Staff Performance.
- **Salary Advances:** Sum of advances issued on dates within the period, including both open and already-applied advances. It measures recorded advance disbursements, not outstanding recoverable advances or an extra payroll expense.

### Finance > Reports > Finance: Profit Flow, Expense Chart and Cash Flow

- **Profit Calculation:** Starts with Revenue, subtracts COGS to show Gross Profit, then subtracts Wastage, Daily Expenses, Contractor Expenses and Payroll to reach Net Profit. Intermediate totals repeat the cards; they are not additional income or expenses to add together.
- **Expense Distribution donut:** Five slices: COGS, Wastage, Daily Expenses, Contractor Expenses and Payroll. Each percentage = category cost ÷ Total Expenses × 100, displayed as a whole percentage. Shows the composition of recognized cost, not cost as a percentage of revenue; excludes inventory purchases, payments and outstanding debt. Zero/negative cost data can make shares misleading, and rounded percentages need not total exactly 100%.
- **Detailed Expenses table:** Repeats the five profit-cost components. Its Total Operational Expenses row actually totals all five, including COGS and payroll; it is not just the Daily Expenses amount. Additional purchase/payment/payable/advance rows are contextual figures and must not all be summed into expenses.
- **Cash In from Sales:** Repeats order revenue using order creation dates, without independently checking receipts, payment dates or payment methods. Despite its label, it is not verified cash collected during the period.
- **Cash-out lines / Known Cash Out:** Supplier Payments + Contractor Payments + Daily Expense Payments + Payroll Payments + Salary Advances. Payment records use transaction dates; Daily Expense Payments assumes the period's ordinary expenses were paid. Only these recorded channels are included; supplierless purchase settlement is omitted. There is no opening/closing cash balance, bank reconciliation or separation of physical cash from card/bank payments. Known Cash Out is not Total Expenses, and this panel is not a complete cash-flow statement.
- **Example:** A period's AFN 1,000 purchase invoice with AFN 400 paid contributes AFN 1,000 Purchase Value and AFN 400 Supplier Payments; its remaining AFN 600 contributes to current Supplier Payables if supplier-linked. It does not add AFN 1,000 to profit expenses: sold recipe ingredients contribute COGS separately. Paying the AFN 600 later reduces debt and appears in that later payment period without recognizing the purchase cost again.

### Finance > Reports > Finance: Detail Tables and Coverage

- **Purchases by Supplier:** Groups period-dated non-Draft purchase invoices. Invoices counts documents; Purchased sums charges; Paid and Balance are current cumulative settlement on those invoices, including payments outside the period. Cash / No Supplier is a separate group. Unlinked purchase movements are absent, so totals can differ from Purchase Value.
- **Purchases by Ingredient:** Period invoice-line quantities in the displayed ingredient unit and historical line values, not quantities consumed or current stock. The Invoices column actually counts lines, not distinct invoices; repeated ingredient lines can increase it within one invoice.
- **Expenses by Contractor:** Groups period service invoices; Invoices counts documents, Expense sums recognized charges, and Paid/Balance reflect their current cumulative settlement, not period payment activity. **Expenses by Service Type:** Groups those invoices' service lines; Lines counts lines, Qty sums entered service quantities and Expense sums line charges. Differing service units are not a common physical measure.
- **Grouping caution:** Supplier, ingredient, contractor and Finance employee groups use names; distinct same-name records can merge, especially across branches. Ingredient quantities should only be compared in compatible units; name grouping does not establish unit conversion.
- **Payroll by Branch / Employee:** Only Finance's selected approval-date/fallback cohort of Approved/Paid payrolls. Payrolls counts records; Cost is earned payroll expense; Paid is cumulative settlement on those records across all payment dates; Balance is their current remaining net salary. Employee Deductions combines ordinary deductions and advance recovery, already reflected in the relevant cost/net calculations; it must not be subtracted again.
- **Payroll Payment History:** Latest 30 transactions dated in the period, newest payment date/entry time first. Amount is one transaction and Period identifies its linked salary period. The Payroll Payments card includes all matching transactions, not only these 30.
- **Payroll Trends:** Table, not a payment trend chart. Groups the selected Finance payroll cohort by salary-period end month: Payrolls counts records, Cost sums earned expense and Net Salary sums salary after ordinary/advance deductions. Month labels may fall outside the approval-date reporting period; no proration occurs.
- **Unpaid Purchase Invoices:** Up to 25 latest purchase-date records among period purchases marked Unpaid/Partially Paid, including supplierless purchases. Total, Paid and Balance are current values; Due is optional, not an overdue filter. This selected-period sample does not cover the all-history supplier-linked population behind Supplier Payables.
- **Unpaid Contractor Invoices:** Up to 25 latest invoice-date Approved/Partially Paid service invoices from all history, not just the period. Current Total/Paid/Balance apply; possible zero-balance Approved invoices can remain listed. It is not a complete payables ledger or due-date-priority list.
- **PDF limits:** Finance PDF keeps full headline totals but shows only the first ten employee groups, payroll-payment rows, contractor groups, service-type groups and unpaid purchase/service invoice rows, and up to twelve payroll trend months. An empty/short detail section does not establish that the related overall debt or activity is zero.

### Finance > Reports > Inventory: Cards and Movement Chart

- **Total Inventory Items:** Current count of ingredient records, active and inactive; not stock quantity, value or items acquired in the period. Ignores report dates.
- **Low Stock Alerts:** Current count of ingredients with available quantity ≤ minimum threshold, including zero/negative and inactive ingredients. This separate alert list is a current snapshot, not restricted to selected dates, and is not automatically refreshed just by changing report dates. It differs from the PDF's active-only, mutually exclusive Low/Critical/Out of Stock categories.
- **Total Movements:** Count of movements created within the period, excluding Order consumption. Includes Purchase, Adjustment, Waste, Production, Production Adjustment and transfers in/out. Counts movement records, not quantity, orders or purchase invoices; it covers more types than the ordinary Stock Movements page's Purchase/Adjustment/Waste list.
- **Purchase Value:** Sum of signed Purchase movement quantities × recorded unit costs, falling back to current ingredient costs, for movement creation times in the period. Not supplier payments or solely invoice-date purchases. Posting dates and invoice-line linkage can make it differ from Finance Purchase Value.
- **Movement Breakdown donut / legend:** Counts each included movement type; slice percentage = type count ÷ Total Movements × 100, rounded to whole percentages. Indicates event frequency, not stock volume, money, waste rate or order consumption. Order movements are excluded; rounded shares need not total exactly 100%.
- **Low Stock Items panel:** Lists the same current alerts with thresholds, not historical low-stock events. Its displayed current-stock value is presently not populated from the supplied quantity field; a blank figure must not be interpreted as zero. No stockout-duration or forecast is calculated.

### Finance > Reports > Inventory: Purchase Analysis and PDF

- **Purchase Cost Intelligence:** Uses non-Draft invoice lines whose purchase date is in the period, not movement creation dates. Per ingredient, quantity is purchased stock units; Average Cost = summed historical line value ÷ summed quantity, a quantity-weighted average rather than the mean of prices. Last Price/Supplier means the latest purchase within the selected period, not necessarily the latest ever or today's ingredient cost. Value sums historical line charges. Displayed averages round to two decimals; unlinked movements are absent.
- **Supplier History:** Groups those period invoice lines by supplier name, including Cash / No Supplier. Invoices counts distinct invoices, Lines counts invoice lines, Qty sums purchased units and Value sums charges rather than payments. Qty across kilograms, litres and pieces is not a meaningful single physical total; name-based grouping can combine different records.
- **Purchase History:** Latest 30 matching invoice lines, newest purchase date/entry time first. Date is invoice purchase date; Qty, Unit Price and Value are historical line values. Multiple rows can belong to one invoice; it is not a complete invoice ledger. Cost/supplier aggregates cover all matching lines, not just those 30.
- **PDF stock status / valuation:** Current, not reconstructed at the selected end date. For active ingredients only: Out of Stock means quantity ≤ 0; Critical means 0 < quantity ≤ half the threshold; Low means half-threshold < quantity ≤ threshold; Healthy means quantity > threshold. Counts are mutually exclusive. Total Inventory Value = sum of active current quantity × current unit cost, not money spent in the period. Full Inventory also lists inactive records as Inactive, so its rows need not match active valuation categories.
- **PDF movement measures:** Purchase Value follows movement dates; Waste Quantity/Cost sum absolute waste quantities and corresponding recorded/fallback costs. Mixed ingredient units must not be interpreted as one physical quantity. Consumption Quantity/Cost are currently fixed at zero because order consumption is excluded, not proof that no ingredients were used. Daily movement trend counts events and signed net quantities on dates with activity, without inserting zero dates; its quantities can mix units. The movement-type section currently shows purchases only.
- **PDF samples / ranking caveat:** Recent Movements includes at most 30 latest movements, including Order consumption despite its exclusion from headline movement counts. PDF purchase-cost/supplier groups are capped at 15 each and purchase history at 20 lines. Top Wasted Ingredients is capped at ten but sorts signed waste totals before showing their absolute values; ordinary negative waste can put smaller losses first. That ranking's cost uses absolute net waste × current ingredient cost, unlike the report's sum of absolute movement quantities at recorded/fallback costs; it is not a reliable largest-loss ranking.

### Finance > Reports > Staff Performance: Cards and Charts

- **Population / dates:** Staff counts, role distribution and salary profiles describe current branch membership/status, not historical headcount or hires during the period. Attendance uses attendance date; orders use creation time; reservation activity uses booking creation time rather than reserved date. Payroll inclusion is whole salary periods overlapping the selected range: start ≤ selected To and end ≥ selected From, without proration. Payment/advance transactions independently use their own dates.
- **Total / Active / Inactive Staff cards:** Total counts all currently assigned profiles. Active counts status Active; Inactive = Total − Active and therefore also includes Resigned, not just status Inactive. A person assigned to multiple branches can appear in each branch report.
- **Attendance Rate card:** Present attendance records ÷ all attendance records in the period × 100; zero with no records. Leave/Absent records are in the denominator; missing attendance days are not automatically counted absent. Display rounds to one decimal. This is a shift-record rate, not unique-person attendance or a percentage of scheduled working days.
- **Present Days / Attendance Records cards:** Present Days counts Present records, not unique people or calendar dates; multiple shifts can contribute separately. Attendance Records counts records across all statuses, not staff headcount.
- **Payroll Cost card:** Earned expense on Approved/Paid payrolls whose salary periods overlap the selected dates: max(gross − ordinary deductions, 0), without subtracting advance recovery. A one-day range can include an entire monthly payroll. Unlike Finance Payroll Cost, approval date is not the period selector.
- **Payroll Paid card:** Sum of salary-payment transactions dated in the period, regardless of whether their linked salary period overlaps. **Outstanding Salaries card:** Current remaining net salary on Approved overlapping-period payrolls only, not all-history salary debt. **Salary Advances card:** Advances issued in the period, including applied and open amounts, not the remaining unrecovered balance.
- **Staff by Role chart:** Count of current scoped staff per stored role; percentage = role count ÷ Total Staff × 100. Includes inactive/resigned profiles; not hours worked, payroll cost or productivity.
- **Attendance Summary chart:** Counts by attendance status, each divided by all period attendance records for its share; same denominator as Attendance Rate. **Daily Attendance table:** Record counts by date/status for dates with recorded attendance; omitted dates do not establish everyone was absent or off duty.

### Finance > Reports > Staff Performance: Role Activity Tables

- **Waiter Performance:** Lists current Waiter-role profiles, including inactive/zero-activity staff. Orders Handled counts distinct period-created dine-in orders attributed to their creator, including unfinished/cancelled orders; Completed Orders counts Completed only, not Delivered. Revenue sums gross line value only for line statuses Pending, Ready, Served or Completed on those orders, with stored-price/current-price fallback. In-progress and other line statuses are excluded, but a cancelled order with eligible lines can still contribute. No discounts, reservation/delivery charges or receipt verification; not the restaurant's successful net sales.
- **Delivery Performance:** Lists current Delivery Boy profiles. Both Deliveries Handled and Delivered count only Delivered delivery orders assigned to that employee, so the two counts currently coincide; they do not measure assignment count or success rate. Revenue uses those orders' full discounted shared order value, not only delivery fees. Creation date, not delivery-completion date, controls inclusion.
- **Cashier Performance:** Lists current Cashier profiles. Reservations counts non-cancelled bookings created by that cashier in the period. Orders Paid counts Completed/Delivered orders received by that cashier and created in the period, rather than payment transactions dated in it.
- **Total Cash Handled:** Current paid amounts on the included non-cancelled bookings, plus estimated settlement on the included received orders. Order settlement = max((non-cancelled item value + non-cancelled stored reservation amount + eligible delivery fee) × (1 − discount%) − prepaid reservation paid amount, 0). Uses the stored reservation amount here, not the shared table-rate charge. This is an attributed estimate, not reconciled till cash, payment-date collections or a cash-only method total; later booking payments can change a historical creation-date report.
- **Comparison / PDF Top Performers:** Role revenues use different rules and can describe the same underlying order; they must not be summed as extra business revenue or treated as equivalent productivity scores. The PDF ranks up to ten active-in-the-report waiter/delivery/cashier rows by their role-specific revenue/cash-handled figure, then order count; this does not establish profit contribution or an objectively comparable staff ranking.

### Finance > Reports > Staff Performance: Salary and History Tables

- **Salary History:** Despite its title, current salary configuration per employee, independent of report dates: salary type, base/rate, payment day, recurring allowances/deductions and payroll-enabled status. Not a timeline of historical salary changes or earned salary in the period; salary-basis meanings follow Staff/Payroll definitions.
- **Payroll Summary:** Groups overlapping-period payrolls by employee, including Drafts. Payrolls counts records; Gross, ordinary Deductions, advance-recovery Advances and Net sum their payroll fields; Balance = summed Net − cumulative Paid. Unlike the Payroll Cost card, draft calculations contribute to these rows. Payrolls can mistakenly display with an AFN format; it remains a record count, not currency.
- **Payroll History:** Latest 100 overlapping salary-period records, including Drafts, ordered by newest period end. Gross, Net and Balance are current payroll amounts, not transaction flows or prorated portions. Aggregate cards/summary are not limited to these 100 rows.
- **Payment History / Advance History:** Each shows at most the latest 100 matching transactions, ordered by transaction date/entry time. Payment Amount is one salary disbursement; its linked salary period can fall outside the selected range. Advance Amount is one issued advance; Applied means linked to payroll, not separately verified recovery, while Open means unlinked. Their headline transaction totals cover all matching records.
- **Employee Earnings:** Per employee, Earnings sums earned expense and Outstanding sums remaining salary for Approved/Paid overlapping payrolls. Paid is cumulative settlement of the overlapping payroll records across all payment dates, not the period's Payroll Paid transactions. Earnings is already after ordinary deductions, but not advance recovery, and is not take-home cash.
- **Employee Deductions:** Sums ordinary deductions plus advance recovery across overlapping payrolls, including Drafts. The Advances column is the advance-recovery subset already included in Deductions, not additional money to subtract or new advances issued during the selected period.
- **PDF coverage:** Current salary profiles, payroll history, payment history, advance history and employee earnings are further limited to the first 25 rows each. The PDF's Employee Deductions summary includes ordinary deductions plus advance recovery, including Drafts; it is not another payroll cost. Full aggregate totals and shorter detail lists can legitimately differ.

### Finance > Reports > Menu Items: Scope, Filters and Cards

- **Sales basis:** Completed/Delivered orders created in the applied Kabul period; cancelled lines and all other order statuses are excluded. Menu items and platters are separate products, even with the same name; one platter is one sellable unit, not its component portions. Gross Sales = sold quantity × recorded order-time price, falling back to current price for legacy lines. Discount attributes the order's percentage to item gross; Net Sales/Revenue = Gross − attributed Discount. Excludes delivery/reservation charges, COGS and profit; not a refund ledger.
- **Filters / ranking scope:** Name search covers English/Dari/Pashto; category uses the product's current category. Selling Only keeps positive-unit products; Zero Sales Only includes catalogue products with no qualifying sales. Include Zero-Sales Items adds current scoped catalogue products, including unavailable ones; otherwise All does not mean the entire catalogue. Top/Bottom 5/10/20/50 selects by Quantity or Revenue before display sorting. All cards, categories, charts, trends and insights use that full filtered/ranked set, not only the table's current 25-row page.
- **Total Menu Items Sold card:** Count of distinct selected products with positive units; not portions, catalogue size or number of orders. Different dish/platter records count separately.
- **Total Units Sold card:** Sum of selected non-cancelled product quantities, with platters counted as platter units. Not unique customers or component servings.
- **Total Menu Item Revenue card / Discount subtitle:** Sum of selected products' net sales; the subtitle sums attributed item discount. Net excludes fees and reservation charges, unlike Orders/Finance Total Revenue. Discount is already subtracted, not an additional deduction from displayed revenue.
- **Average Revenue Per Item card:** Selected net revenue ÷ distinct selling products, or zero if none. Average contribution per selling product, not average order value or price per unit.
- **Average Selling Price card:** Selected net revenue ÷ total units, or zero if none. Quantity-weighted realized price after discount, not the arithmetic average of menu prices or today's list price.
- **Top / Lowest Selling Item cards:** Highest quantity and lowest positive quantity among selling products in the selected set, with revenue/name tie-breaks. Show that product's units and net revenue. Lowest excludes zero-sales products even when included in the table; neither card switches to highest/lowest revenue merely because ranking is by Revenue.

### Finance > Reports > Menu Items: Tables, Charts, Insights and Export

- **Sales Details table:** Rank is position after the current display sort, not a permanent popularity rank. Item type distinguishes platters; Category reflects current classification. Orders counts distinct qualifying orders containing that product; adding product order counts double-counts orders containing multiple products. Gross, Discount and Net follow the definitions above; Average Price = product Net ÷ units. Table count includes selected zero-sales rows, unlike the distinct selling-products card; Net Total covers the entire selection, not only the current page.
- **% of Sales column:** Product net sales ÷ all filtered-scope net sales before Top/Bottom selection × 100; zero when the denominator is zero. Top/Bottom rows need not add to 100%, even though the headline revenue describes only the selected ranking set.
- **Cancelled Qty column:** Cancelled lines within otherwise successful orders, not units on wholly cancelled orders. Products with only cancelled lines can appear as zero-sales catalogue rows with cancelled quantity zero; that zero is not proof there were no cancellations. Cancelled quantities do not add to Units Sold or sales revenue.
- **Category Performance table:** Groups the selected product set by current category, or Uncategorized. Items Sold counts distinct selling products; Units/Gross/Discount/Net sum product values. Average Item Revenue = category Net ÷ distinct selling products. Category % of Sales instead divides by the selected ranking set's total Net, so its denominator differs from the product table's pre-ranking share. No cost/margin inference follows from a category's revenue share.
- **Top Selling Items bar chart:** Up to ten positive-selling products within the current selection, always ranked by quantity. Bar length is Units Sold, not AFN, profit or the chosen Revenue ranking metric. In a Bottom selection it shows the highest quantities among that bottom subset, not the restaurant's overall top sellers.
- **Revenue by Category donut:** Positive category net revenue within the selected filtered/ranked set. Slice share measures category Net ÷ displayed positive category Net, not all restaurant sales, gross charges or profit. Zero-net categories are omitted.
- **Sales Trend:** Bars show product units on the left axis; the line shows net AFN revenue on the right. Uses the selected products over the applied order-creation period, not only the visible table page. Daily buckets for up to 45 inclusive dates, weekly for 46–240 and monthly above 240; weekly buckets start Monday, so a clipped first week's label may precede the selected start. Only buckets with activity are shown; missing dates are not filled with zero and no forecast/growth comparison is calculated.
- **Insights:** Top Performer means highest net revenue; Most Sold means highest units; Category Leader means highest category net revenue; Low Performer means lowest positive units, excluding unsold products. These are rule-based summaries of the selected set, not AI forecasts, profit rankings or explanations of why demand changed.
- **History / rounding:** Product names/categories and legacy fallback prices are current, so renaming, recategorization or repricing can change historical presentation. Product discount/net totals are rounded to cents; independently rounded products, trend buckets and other reports can have small differences.
- **PDF configuration / coverage:** The Menu Items PDF has its own dates, category, ranking/limit, zero-sales inclusion, display sort and section choices; defaults are copied from much of the screen state, but the screen's name search and Selling/Zero Sales Only filter are not passed through. Export can therefore cover a different product population. It includes the complete configured selection rather than only the table's 25-row page; Top/Bottom limits still intentionally reduce that selection.

### Administration > User Activity

- **Purpose / scope:** Monitors authenticated staff accounts and sign-in sessions, not attendance, hours worked, sales productivity or a record of every user action. Admin defaults to All Branches, independently of the working branch; Branch Admin is restricted to the assigned branch. Monitoring permission and session-revocation authority are separate; Branch Admin can monitor but does not automatically have revocation authority.
- **Active Users:** Distinct accounts with at least one currently Active session in scope. Multiple active devices/sign-ins for the same account count as one user; not the number of employees whose employment status is Active.
- **Live Sessions:** Number of valid sessions currently Active or Idle. Counts sign-ins, not unique people or necessarily distinct physical devices; an account can have several sessions. Offline but valid sessions are excluded from this card even though they remain in the Live Sessions table.
- **Idle Users:** Distinct accounts with an Idle session and no Active session in scope. An account active on one session and idle on another counts as Active, not Idle. Active Users + Idle Users measures distinct online accounts, not Live Sessions.
- **Valid Accounts:** Distinct accounts with at least one session not logged out, revoked or past its authentication expiry, including Offline sessions. Not all registered accounts, a licence quota, enabled employee count or evidence that anyone is currently working. It can exceed the combined Active/Idle user counts.
- **Online by Role:** For each recorded session role, Users counts distinct accounts with Active/Idle sessions; Sessions counts those online sessions. Includes Idle users, unlike Active Users alone. Role is recorded on the session, not necessarily the employee's latest role after a later profile change; an account with different recorded roles can appear in multiple role groups.
- **Presence statuses:** Defaults: Active means a connection update within 90 seconds and interaction within five minutes; Idle means a recent connection but interaction more than five minutes ago; Offline means no recent connection or no available observation. Thresholds are configurable. Passive reading can be Idle; hidden/closed browsers, connection loss or observation failures can look Offline without logout or session expiry. These statuses are not attendance judgments.
- **Filters / coverage:** Search matches staff name/username; role and status filters narrow table rows/counts, not headline cards or role summaries. Branch selection changes both. Newest sign-in first; all matching sessions are paginated, normally 20 per page, without a 100-record total cap. There is no date-range selector or default current-month restriction.

### Administration > User Activity: Sessions and Security History

- **Live Sessions / Security History:** Live lists valid sessions, including those currently Offline. Security History includes both valid and ended sessions, not only security incidents; Logout, Revoked and Expired identify different reasons a session ended. A row represents a sign-in session, not an audit event, failed-login attempt or every activity performed during it. History has the same search/role/branch/status filters and full pagination, but no headline cards.
- **Concurrent count:** Number of all valid sessions for that account within the authorized branch scope, including Offline sessions and records outside table search/role/status filters. Not just the visible page, online devices or distinct hardware. Ended history rows can show the account's current concurrent count rather than its count when that old session ended.
- **Role / branch / device / session:** Role and browser/operating-system descriptors identify the recorded sign-in; Session is a shortened session reference, not an employee ID. Branch is the session association, not every branch assigned to the employee; switching branches can update the same session's association. A missing association displays All Branches but does not establish unrestricted permissions.
- **Started / Last Seen:** Started is sign-in time; routine authentication renewal keeps the same session rather than starting another. Last Seen is the latest available connection observation, not the last business transaction; ended sessions can fall back to their ending time. Never Seen can mean the observation is no longer available, not that the account was never used. Relative times and older displayed timestamps use the device clock/timezone; this is not a permanent minute-by-minute presence history.
- **Live Updates / Polling Fallback:** Describes the monitoring page's connection, not an employee's presence or account validity. Updates can lag: the default periodic refresh is two minutes with live updates connected, otherwise one minute. Connection/observation failures can leave stale or understated online counts; zero online users does not prove no one is operating the restaurant.
- **Revocation meaning:** Ends eligible sign-in sessions and requires a new login; it does not delete staff, change employment/payroll status or reset the account password. Revoke All covers that account's valid sessions within the authorized restaurant/branch scope, not necessarily every branch/device globally; it can include the administrator's own session. It is not reversible session restoration.

### Administration > Feedbacks

- **Purpose / scope:** Customer review cards for the selected branch, including rating, comment, review target and restaurant response. Loads all scoped review history without a 100-record limit; there are no page-level total/average-rating cards, trend charts or pagination controls. The Dashboard's Rating has its separate all-time definition; these filters do not change that dashboard figure.
- **Rating / customer:** Each review has a 1–5 rating; filled stars and the number repeat that one rating, not an average or satisfaction percentage. Customer label uses the linked account username rather than a verified personal name; it can be missing for a customer without a linked account. A blank comment does not invalidate a rating.
- **Menu Item / Delivery / General:** Menu Item identifies the linked dish; Delivery identifies a linked order; General has neither target. If both links exist, the card displays Menu Item first. There is no direct platter-review target. These labels do not independently verify a completed purchase, successful delivery or one review per customer/order; duplicate submissions can affect review-based averages.
- **All / Responded / Not Responded:** Determined by whether response text exists, not rating, response date, complaint severity or resolution. Responded means a reply is recorded, not that the customer accepted it or the issue was resolved. Existing replies have no on-page edit/delete controls; a reply does not create a refund, discount or order-status change.
- **Date filter limitation:** Applies only when both dates exist; a lone From or To has no effect. Filters review creation time, not response time. Bounds are exact UTC midnights, not complete inclusive Kabul days: most reviews on the selected end date are excluded, and early-Kabul reviews on the start date can also be excluded. Equal From/To matches only the exact midnight instant. No default date restriction or guaranteed newest-first ordering is supplied.
- **Review Date / Responded On:** Display calendar dates in the device's timezone. Responded On can use a browser-supplied timestamp rather than strictly server-confirmed receipt time; it is not a reliable response-time/SLA measure. Filtering creation dates does not identify replies made during that period.
- **Interpretation / empty state:** Reviews are customer-reported opinions, not a representative survey of all guests, revenue or employee performance. No Reviews Found can mean no filter matches or a loading failure, not necessarily no feedback exists. The date-boundary and response-time rules above describe current behavior; the intended business policy is unverified.

### Administration > Branches

- **Purpose / scope:** Restaurant-wide branch directory and configuration overview, available in the Admin portal rather than Branch Admin's branch-restricted menu. Includes active and inactive branches, independently of the current working branch; main branch first, then name. No date, search or historical-headcount filter. This page is not a branch revenue/profit comparison.
- **Branches Used / Maximum / Remaining:** Used counts all current branches, including Main and inactive branches; Maximum is the subscription's configured branch allowance; Remaining is allowance minus Used, with Limit Reached shown when nonpositive. These are current subscription-capacity figures, not operating capacity or branches opened during a period. Inactivation does not free a slot; a new additional branch requires a valid subscription and available quota.
- **Staff column:** Count of current branch-assigned staff profiles, including inactive/resigned employees and automatically assigned administrator profiles. Not staff currently online, attending or working only at that branch. The same employee can appear in several branch counts; adding counts can exceed unique restaurant headcount. Admin profiles are automatically assigned to active branches.
- **Main / Active / Inactive:** Main is the designated primary branch, not the most profitable/largest one; only one is permitted and it must remain active. Active means enabled, distinct from the current active branch used as a working context. Inactivation retains branch records/history and is not liquidation or settlement of obligations; ordinary selection/public availability is restricted to active branches.
- **Branch code / contacts:** Code is unique within the restaurant and normalized to uppercase, not a performance rank or count. Contact/location details describe the branch; their presence does not verify business activity or customer reach.
- **Public Menu / QR:** Link and QR are customer menu-entry references, not sales, scan counts or proof that ordering is available. Currently every branch's generated link/QR uses the same restaurant-level menu entry point rather than directly selecting its individual branch, despite separate branch labels/files. Regeneration does not create or change an order.
- **Deactivation / deletion limits:** Main cannot be deactivated or deleted. Deactivation is blocked if any assigned employee other than Admin/SuperAdmin would have no other active assigned branch; Branch Admin is not exempt. Deletion is restricted by linked records and staff assignments; setup, audit, migration or session history can matter even with no sales. Current staff eligibility checking can also block deletion when assigned staff already have other branches, including an automatically assigned Admin; intended handling of that case remains unverified. Deletion is not the archival alternative to inactivation, and no restore function is supplied.

### Administration > Branches: Configuration Migration

- **Purpose / direction:** Copies configuration from another active branch in the same restaurant into the current working branch; source and destination must differ. Not physical stock transfer, branch consolidation, financial-history migration or continuous synchronization. The branch directory is restaurant-wide, but the migration destination follows the current working branch.
- **Copy types:** Ingredients copies names, stock units, unit costs, minimum thresholds and activity flags; newly copied ingredient quantities start at zero. Menu Categories copies category information. Menu Items also includes category, ingredient and recipe dependencies, prices, descriptions and availability/production-tracking flags. Platters additionally includes component dishes and quantities. Everything combines these supported menu/configuration copies, not every restaurant record.
- **Important exclusions:** No stock quantities, prepared-production batches, stock movements, purchases, transfers, ledgers or order/payment history are copied. Kitchen-station definitions/assignments are not copied. A copied production-tracking flag supplies no prepared portions; availability still follows Restaurant > Menu and Operations > Daily Productions. Copying ingredient costs is not an inventory receipt or expense.
- **Existing matches / legacy data:** Category/ingredient matches use trimmed case-insensitive names; dish/platter matches also use the destination category. Matches are reused rather than overwritten, preserving existing destination prices, ingredient stock and recipe quantities; missing recipe links can still be added. Repeating a copy is not synchronization of later source edits. For each data kind, restaurant-wide legacy definitions are a fallback only when that source branch has none of its own, not automatically merged with all source-branch definitions.
- **Imported / Skipped / Failed:** Counts configuration records/recipes across all included dependency steps, not sold units, stock amounts or distinct menu products; platter component links are not counted separately. Skipped normally means an existing match was reused, not rejected/failed work. Modifiers is a placeholder with no supported modifier data and contributes a skipped step; unit/ingredient-category labels do not create separate editable catalogues.
- **Running / Completed / Failed:** Running means the copy has not finished. Completed means processing finished, not that all menu items are sellable or all relationships succeeded; inspect Failed count as well. A fatal failure rolls back that run's copied data and records Failed with a failure count of one, not necessarily one bad source record; retained step summaries can describe attempted work rather than committed changes. No on-page undo is supplied.
- **History coverage:** Latest 50 migration runs across the restaurant, newest start time first, not just the destination currently selected and not a full all-history ledger. Source/Destination identify each run; Imported/Skipped describe that run rather than current catalogue totals. No history date filter or pagination beyond those 50 entries is provided.

### Administration > Settings: Scope and Inheritance

- **Purpose / sections:** Current configuration, not a financial dashboard or historical-period report. Admin sees General, Branding, Location, Delivery, Discounts, Branch, Public Links and Social. Branch Admin sees only Branch settings for the assigned branch; restaurant-wide defaults remain outside that role's Settings scope.
- **Restaurant versus branch:** General/Branding/Location/Delivery/Discounts/Social are restaurant-wide; switching the working branch does not turn them into branch-specific values. Branch settings instead follow the current working branch. A restaurant default affects branches that inherit it, not branches with their own overrides.
- **Inheritance:** For branch coordinates and delivery settings, blank means use the restaurant default; an explicit zero or Disabled is a real override, not missing data. Fallback is per field: a branch can inherit its base fee but override its per-kilometre rate, or combine one branch coordinate with the other restaurant coordinate. Raw blank branch inputs need not show the effective value used for quotes.
- **Saved versus displayed:** Unsaved field/image previews are not effective business configuration. Each section concerns its own settings, not a whole-page financial transaction. Blank restaurant fields currently preserve existing saved values rather than reliably clearing them; blank nullable branch overrides restore inheritance. There is no effective-from date, scheduled change or historical-settings selector.
- **Unavailable controls:** No Receipt tab is registered. Receipt templates, tax/service-charge rates, printer and cash-drawer settings are not mounted controls in the current Settings sections. Stored configuration elsewhere does not establish hardware integration or automatic charges; ordinary order/revenue totals do not add branch tax/service-charge rates, as defined under Dashboard > Shared Sales Meaning.

### Administration > Settings > General

- **Name / Slogan:** Restaurant-wide brand identity, not branch names, menu categories or a performance measure. Name changes can change the public menu address and regenerate restaurant/branch QR files; previously distributed links or printed QR codes do not update themselves. Existing business record IDs are not new transactions because the brand was renamed.
- **Phone / Email / Address / Website:** Restaurant contact and public-information defaults, not employee login details, branch-specific contacts, delivery eligibility or revenue. Website is a supplied external address, distinct from the generated restaurant menu link in Public Links. Changing textual Address does not automatically update geographic coordinates.

### Administration > Settings > Branding

- **Logo / Cover Image:** Restaurant-wide brand images used by public/customer displays; not separate branch logos, menu-product images or QR codes. The logo identifies the restaurant and the cover provides its larger promotional image; neither measures reach, ratings or sales.
- **Preview / limits:** A selected preview is not proof that the stored public image changed. Existing saved images remain until replaced; this section has no explicit stored-image removal control. Branding changes do not publish a new application build or alter order charges.

### Administration > Settings > Location

- **Latitude / Longitude:** Restaurant's geographic delivery-origin defaults, not its written address or a customer's destination. Customer delivery distance uses branch coordinates where supplied, with restaurant fallback for each missing coordinate. Both effective coordinates are needed for location-based customer delivery; changing address text does not geocode them.
- **Distance meaning:** Customer distance is a straight-line geographic estimate between the effective origin and delivery address, not a mapped road route, travel time or actual rider mileage. Staff-entered delivery orders use their entered distance instead, so the same destination need not produce identical charges across those entry methods.
- **Validation limitation:** Restaurant and branch configuration currently do not enforce geographic coordinate bounds or require a complete coordinate pair. Numeric values alone do not establish a correct location; intended location safeguards are unverified.

### Administration > Settings > Delivery

- **Delivery Available:** Restaurant default for whether delivery orders may be accepted, unless explicitly overridden by the branch. A branch Enabled/Disabled value takes precedence, including Disabled when the restaurant default is enabled. This does not deactivate the branch or disable takeaway/dine-in, assign a driver or promise driver capacity.
- **Delivery Radius:** Maximum permitted distance in kilometres for delivery validation. Distance equal to the radius is allowed; greater distance is rejected. Customer ordering uses the geographic estimate above, while staff delivery validation uses the entered distance. Not a delivery-time promise or automatically drawn road-service area.
- **Base Delivery Fee / Price per km:** Estimated delivery charge = effective base fee in AFN + distance in km × effective AFN rate per km. Fractional distance is used, not rounded-up whole kilometres; saved/quoted money is rounded to cents. Base fee applies once per delivery order, not per product or kilometre. Zero base fee removes only the fixed part; delivery is free only when the resulting whole formula is zero.
- **Minimum Order:** Required dish/platter subtotal in AFN for a delivery order, before order discounts and excluding delivery/reservation charges. Equality qualifies. It is an eligibility threshold, not an extra charge; delivery fees cannot bring an otherwise insufficient item subtotal up to the minimum. It is not applied as a general takeaway/dine-in minimum.
- **Effect on charges:** Defaults/overrides affect new validations and quotes; they do not automatically reprice saved delivery fees on existing orders. The fee is an order charge, not a recorded rider payment or ordinary expense. Subsequent order discount/revenue treatment follows Dashboard > Shared Sales Meaning; a configured rate alone creates no revenue or cash collection.
- **Validation limitation:** Settings currently permit negative fees/rates, radius and minimum amounts; there is no enforced nonnegative business bound. These values should not be interpreted as approved refunds, credits or special delivery policies. Intended safeguards are unverified; the formulas above describe the actual calculation rather than validating the configuration's business suitability.

### Administration > Settings > Discounts

- **Manager / Admin Discount Limits:** Restaurant-wide percentage thresholds for manual discount-request decision authority, not AFN amounts, automatic customer discounts or branch-specific limits. New restaurant defaults are 10% and 100% respectively; existing restaurants use their saved values. Authority bands, request limits and charge replacement are defined under Operations > Discount Requests.
- **Current versus historical effect:** Decision checks use current limits, including for requests already waiting. Changing a limit does not itself apply a discount, remove an already approved order percentage or reprice historical sales. Discount-card percentages follow their separate entitlement/application rules rather than these manual-request bands.
- **Ordering / range limitation:** Settings do not enforce 0–100% values or Manager limit ≤ Admin limit. The Manager check is evaluated first, so a higher Manager limit can permit a request above the lower Admin limit; Admin is not an unconditional ceiling in that configuration. A request above both limits currently cannot even be rejected through the normal decision rule. Intended configuration constraints are unverified.

### Administration > Settings > Branch

- **Override fields:** Current working branch's latitude/longitude, delivery availability, radius, base fee, price per km and minimum order. The same financial/distance definitions above apply; blank numeric overrides and Use Restaurant Default inherit, while zero and Disabled override. No branch-specific discount-limit, branding or social fields are mounted here; this section does not change branch name, quota or employment assignments.
- **Opening Hours:** Free-text public information, not a parsed weekly schedule, employee shift plan or automatic time-of-day cutoff. Blank branch text falls back to restaurant opening-hours text for the public open-status check; the current General section does not expose a restaurant opening-hours input.
- **Open / Closed interpretation:** After ignoring case and repeated spaces, explicit English values Open, Open Now, Always Open, 24/7 or 24 Hours mean open; Closed or Closed Today mean closed. An inactive branch is closed. Other text, including ordinary time ranges such as 09:00–21:00, means unknown rather than automatically open/closed according to the clock. It is not a measured service-status KPI.
- **Ordering implication:** Explicitly closed branches are rejected by customer marketplace checkout. Ordinary time-range text does not enforce that schedule, and this flag is not a universal lock on staff order entry. An enabled delivery setting alone does not override customer checks for an inactive/closed branch, valid subscription, product availability, location, radius or minimum order.

### Administration > Settings > Public Links

- **Brand URL / QR:** Generated customer menu-entry address and its QR image, not the external Website field, a unique visitor count or ordering-performance metric. Open Website here opens that generated menu address. Copying/opening/downloading a reference does not publish a new site or create a transaction.
- **Branch Overview / count:** Full current restaurant branch list, including inactive branches; the badge counts loaded branches, not active locations, remaining subscription slots or scans. Active/Inactive is the stored branch configuration status. The list has no date filter or 100-record cap, but a loading failure can leave an empty count; it is not proof the restaurant has no branches.
- **Branch references:** Individual rows show links and available QR files even for inactive records. The common restaurant-level destination and branch-QR limitation are defined under Administration > Branches; a branch-labelled image/link does not guarantee direct selection of that branch or customer-order eligibility. No scan analytics or QR-attributed sales are calculated here.

### Administration > Settings > Social

- **Facebook / Instagram / X:** Restaurant-wide public profile URLs, not usernames for staff access, connected social accounts, follower counts or campaign results. Website is configured separately under General; these fields identify external destinations.
- **Effect / limits:** Saving a URL changes stored public contact information; it does not post content, message customers, import reviews or establish ownership of the linked account. Blank entries currently do not reliably clear previously saved restaurant links, as described under Settings: Scope and Inheritance.
